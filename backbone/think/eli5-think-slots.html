premonition v2 · Think-1

Silent Think Slots

A small add-on that lets a borrowed AI think quietly on 16 hidden sticky notes before it answers.

the problem

Thinking out loud helps, but it is slow

On 40 store-helper jobs, the model got 30 right when it wrote its thoughts and 23 when it did not.

what we tried first

Re-running the brain's middle made it worse

Looping the model's own middle layers knocked it off balance, and 12 loops made it much worse.

the new idea

Sticky notes that go around and around

Each lap, the 16 notes pass through the whole model, and a tiny helper rewrites them.

safety

A closed door keeps it safe

At the start the answer cannot see the notes, so it begins exactly as good as before; training opens the door slowly.

how it was picked

Six designs, one winner

Six designers, three judges and six attackers; every judge ranked the sticky-note design first.

the fair test

It must beat both by 3 and improve with laps

On a small 0.8B model first: about 8,700 village questions it never trained on, three seeds per method.

Checks that it is the laps doing the work: the same helper trained for only 1 lap, notes swapped between questions, and plain training run as long.

what happens next

One cheap trial first; only a pass earns the big model

If the first seed scores under 68, we stop early and save the money.
