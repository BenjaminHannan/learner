Think-1 · the night of Oct 24–25

Think-1 Test Night

We tested whether letting a small AI scribble silent notes and think for 8 laps before answering makes it smarter. It didn't.

The result

Studying longer beat thinking harder

Both got the same 1,254 extra practice steps. Spent on the notes they gained almost nothing; spent on plain study, 4 points.

The key check

More laps, same score

If thinking helped, the line would climb with each lap. It stayed flat on every seed.

Were the notes even used?

Shuffling its notes changed almost nothing

We swapped each question's notes with another question's. A model that relied on them would drop a lot.

What went wrong overnight

A rough night for the rented computers

The first machine never sent its files back, money nearly ran out, and three new boxes were broken on arrival.

The bill

It cost about $14, far more than I said

Each seed ran about twice as long as I planned, and the bad machine wasted $2.34.

What happens next

Next time: one cheap seed first

A trial of about $3 would have shown this flat line. Only a real gain should unlock the full test.
