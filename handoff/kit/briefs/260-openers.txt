Why (the director's probe on 138m, 17:50). All names here are fictional.

  - "Oh, and Pell's boss is Rhoda."

  - "Okay so Brann's sister is Liesl."

The one change: openers and greetings are never part of a fact.
1. One closed list of openers, fixed before any panel read. Build it from:
   - the union of DISCOURSE137B and FILLER_OPENERS;
   - greetings (hi, hey, hello, hiya, good morning/afternoon/evening, with an optional "there", "again" or "Premonition");
   - common multi-word openers ("oh and", "okay so", "and also", "fun fact", "just so you know", "for the record", "one more thing", …);
   - hesitation words (um, uh, er).
   It is a closed-class list; take care with words that are also names (Will, Hope, Joy, May, Art, Bill, Mark, Pat, Grace, Sunny, Faith, Rose).
2. A turn that starts with a listed opener, followed by a comma, "!", "." or "-", or directly by a clause the head can read, is handled as the rest of the turn.
   - Strip at most 2 openers ("Oh, and so, …").
   - Use the rest only if the head gives it a non-clarify action (a save, an answer, or a fixed reply). Otherwise the original turn goes to the head unchanged.
   - Keep the user's words otherwise: don't lowercase or re-case names.
3. A bare greeting (the whole turn is listed greeting words plus optional "there", "again" or "Premonition" and punctuation) gets the head's own greeting reply, exactly the reply the head gives to "Hello.".
4. Write guard, belt and braces. No stored subject may start with a listed opener followed by a comma. A turn that would store one is handled by step 2, or, if step 2 doesn't apply, by the head's own save-failure reply with 0 writes.
5. Safety:
   - Questions never write.
   - The user's name keeps its confirm flow.
   - Inferred facts are never stored.
   - Names or titles that start with a listed word WITHOUT a following comma or "!" ("Hey Jude", "So Long Summer", "Will Tarn") must not be stripped when they are the subject or value. When you can't tell, don't strip.
6. Everything else stays byte-identical.
