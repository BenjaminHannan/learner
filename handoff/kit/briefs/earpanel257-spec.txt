Schema
- Each line is exactly {id, family, turn, gold, clear, notes}.
  - id: "e257-001" … "e257-150", in family-block order.
  - turn: one user chat turn.
  - gold: a list of frames. [] means nothing may be saved.
  - clear: bool. false when a second reading is also defensible.
  - notes: string with tags.
- TEACH frame: exactly {act: "TEACH", subject, relation, relation_aliases, value}.
- ASK frame: exactly {act: "ASK", subject, relation, chain, relation_aliases, chain_aliases}.
  - chain: null for one hop. For two hops it is [first relation, second relation]; relation is then the second one and subject is the start person.
  - chain_aliases: null for one hop. For two hops it is [aliases of hop 1, aliases of hop 2]; the second list equals relation_aliases.
- subject and value are the exact words from the turn, in the same case. "me" stands for the speaker.
- relation is a plain English snake_case name (city, employer, workplace, job, boss, sister, spouse, language, hometown, place_of_birth, pet, school …). relation_aliases lists accepted alternative names.
- Keep one fixed alias list per relation in the generator.

Risk quotas. Tag each item in notes. All wordings are new; never copy an example in this spec word for word.
- R1, pronoun reference across clauses: at least 10 turns, spread over varied_teach and full_names.
  - A later clause uses she/he/they/it for a named third person.
  - At least 3 of these have the pronoun refer to the speaker's relative while a first-person clause is also present.
  - Gold subjects are the resolved names.
- R2, questions shaped like statements: at least 10 turns in no_save, gold [].
  - Include tag questions, a rising "so …?", and doubled "??".
  - At least 6 of them have NO question mark at all (lowercase chat style).
  - Plus at least 3 statements that contain a question word but state a fact. These go in varied_teach with a TEACH gold.
- R3, the verb decides the relation, not a noun in the value: at least 8 turns in varied_teach and full_names.
  - Teaching or coaching at a School, College or Club means workplace; studying at one means school.
  - Mark clear: false only where two relations are really defensible.
- R4, compound family relations (step-, half-, -in-law, grand-, great-, second cousin): at least 8 items across the teach families and questions. Gold keeps the full relation (e.g. sister_in_law, step_brother), with no plain-relative alias.
- R5, everyday relations beyond the core list: at least 10 items across all families. Examples: housemates, instruments, favourite foods or sports, hobbies, teachers, doctors, neighbours.
- R6, two-hop questions whose first hop is a relative: at least 6 of the chain_questions.
- The rest spreads over:
  - plain facts, pets, jobs, languages and birthplaces;
  - full names of 2–4 words;
  - lowercase and typo'd chat: at least 15 turns in all;
  - corrections, which hold only the new value (a negated old value is never in gold).
- questions and chain_questions include at least 5 lowercase or no-"?" questions.
