Schema. Same as ear panel 235, whose README (artifacts/claude-earpanel235-20260922/README.md) the writer may read. The writer must NOT read that panel's panel.jsonl or make_panel.py.
- Each line is exactly {id, family, turn, gold, clear, notes}.
  - id: "e235b-001" … "e235b-150", in family-block order.
  - turn: one user chat turn.
  - gold: a list of frames; [] means nothing may be saved.
  - clear: bool. false when a second reading is also defensible.
  - notes: string.
- TEACH frame: exactly {act: "TEACH", subject, relation, relation_aliases, value}.
- ASK frame: exactly {act: "ASK", subject, relation, chain, relation_aliases}.
  - chain is null for one hop.
  - For two hops, chain = [first relation, second relation], relation = the second one, and subject = the start person.
- subject and value are the exact words from the turn, in the same case. "me" stands for the speaker.
- relation is a plain English snake_case name (city, employer, workplace, job, boss, sister, spouse, language, hometown, place_of_birth, pet …). relation_aliases lists accepted alternative names. Keep one fixed alias list in the generator, so the same relation always gets the same aliases.

The diagnosed risks get dedicated items. All wordings are new; don't copy any example below word for word.
- R1, pronoun reference across clauses: at least 10 turns, spread over varied_teach and full_names.
  - A second clause uses she/he/they/it for a named third person ("My cousin is X and she …", "X started at Y last week, he …").
  - At least 3 turns where the pronoun refers to the speaker's relative and a first-person clause is also present.
  - Gold subjects are the resolved names, which appear in the turn.
- R2, questions shaped like statements: at least 10 turns in no_save, gold [].
  - Tag questions ("X lives in Y, right?", "…, doesn't she?"), rising "so X works at Y?", "X speaks Y??", and a question with no "?" in lowercase chat style.
  - Plus at least 3 statements that contain a question word but state a fact ("I know who X's boss is: it's Y."). These are not no_save; their gold is the TEACH frame, placed in varied_teach.
- R3, the verb decides the relation, not a noun in the value: at least 8 turns in varied_teach and full_names.
  - "teaches at X School" means workplace; "studies at Y College" means school; "coaches at Z Club" means workplace; "works at the W Library" means employer or workplace.
  - Mark clear: false where two relations are really defensible, and list both in relation_aliases only when both are really right.
- The rest spreads over plain facts, relatives, pets, jobs, languages, birthplaces, full names with 2–4 words, lowercase and typo'd chat, and corrections. Corrections hold only the new value; a negated old value must never be in gold.
- questions and chain_questions: new wordings, including verb forms and possessives, and at least 5 lowercase or no-"?" questions.
