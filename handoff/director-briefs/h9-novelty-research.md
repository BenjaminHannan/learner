# H9: what would make the reasoner genuinely novel?
Ben's rule: the reasoner must be NOVEL ("a transformer reader, thinker and talker isn't novel"), decide its own thinking time (learned stop), never be told the puzzle kind, run no hand-written rules, and learn new kinds from few examples; Ben rejects anything built for mazes. Current reasoner: looped transformer, 2 shared blocks, width 256 (1,645,726 weights), learned stop, cap 48 rounds. It beats a same-size plain net on the few-example ruler (artifacts/claude-fewex-20260927/RESULTS-EQ.md: F_eq 51.00/51.29 vs plain 33.79/33.58) but a looped transformer is not itself novel.
