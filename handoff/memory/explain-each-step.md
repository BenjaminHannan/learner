On 2026-09-19, during autonomous experiment screens, Ben said: "explain to me what you're doing. I want to understand each step. Each time you do something, explain it to me in a way I as a high school senior would understand."

**Why:** Premonition is his project and he wants to actually understand it, not just receive results (see [[human-learning-redesign]]: co-design step by step). Terse status tables with jargon (seeds, held-out, LR cooldown, parity test) don't do that.

**How to apply:** For every step, say in plain words: what I'm doing, why, what I expect, what came back, and what it means for the next step. Define each technical term the first time with an everyday analogy (keep using the Mira/Oren shoes example for two-hop questions). Keep numbers, but attach meaning to them. Still obey [[focused-priorities-and-claims]]: claims no stronger than the evidence.

**Update 2026-09-20:** Ben asked again: "For all results of tests, explain to me what it means simply." Every reported result must come with a plain-English "what this means" (and "what it doesn't mean") — not just numbers, cell names or prediction IDs. Lead with the meaning; put the numbers after.
