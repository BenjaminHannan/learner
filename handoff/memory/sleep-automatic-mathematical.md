Sleep must be an automatic, mathematical process. The model never proposes the rule and never decides what to store. It should "find a meaning" out of all its raw practice (his example: lots of multiplication practice) and use that to improve itself.

**Why:** Ben rejected the GPT sleep review's INDUCE idea ("model proposes a rule, checker verifies") and the CardFold-style software lesson search. He wants the consolidation itself to be the mechanism, like a brain, not a program that writes lessons.

**How to apply:** design sleep experiments as replay of raw experience under compression pressure (weight decay / grokking-style, distillation into a smaller bottleneck), with what gets stored/replayed chosen by a fixed formula (e.g. prediction error, surprise), never by model output or a rule-search program.
