# Note for Ben's uncle (draft for Ben to send, edit freely)

Subject: Could you help with a school-and-research project? (10 minutes)

Hi [Uncle's name],

I'm building a small AI assistant that runs on an ordinary home computer, and I'd love to test it on something real instead of made-up puzzles. Would you be willing to send me about 30 real questions or chores from running your business?

Just the things you actually ask, look up, write, work out, or wish someone would do for you. Rough, messy wording is best. Write them the way you'd ask a person. Some kinds, to jog your memory:

- Something you had to figure out or double-check (a quote, a schedule, a price, a policy)
- Something you had to write (an email, a reply to a customer, a note to staff)
- Something you had to find in your own records (an old invoice, what a customer said last month)
- Something you had to decide between, or plan
- Something you weren't sure of and had to look up

A few things that make this work well:

- Please don't clean the questions up or ask an AI to write them. Real is what matters.
- Leave out private details: real customer names, phone numbers, account numbers, passwords. Swap them for a made-up name or "a customer". If a question only makes sense with private records, still send the question and just say what records it would need.
- It's fine if some are easy and some are hard. Send the ones where you'd say "I wish I didn't have to do this."
- Send them as one plain text message or email, one per line. I'll take it from there.

What I'll do with them: they become a sealed test. I lock them away and never train the assistant on them, so the test stays fair. It only gets used to see how well the assistant does compared with other AIs of the same size, and I'll show you the results. Nothing gets shared outside this project, and I'll delete them if you ever ask.

No deadline. Thank you, it helps a lot.

Ben
