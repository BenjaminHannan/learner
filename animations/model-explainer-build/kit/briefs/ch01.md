# ch01 — One question's trip (kicker "Part 1 of 14", title "One question, start to finish", accent thinker)
Target: 14-18 scenes, 330-420 s of scenes. This is the centrepiece "how it works" chapter: follow ONE question through every stage, slowly, with real text on screen.

Example (made up by the project, label it so): "Tom has 12 apples. He gives away 5, then buys 3. How many does he have?" (81 characters? count them yourself: the deep-dive page says real questions average 81 characters; do NOT claim this one is 81 unless you count it).
Flow to animate (FINISHED-MODEL sec. 3 is the script; storyboards file idea 1 is a short version of the same):
1. The question appears letter by letter (S.letters row or typed text; it is long: use two lines).
2. READ: the borrowed reader reads the whole question once; every letter gets a meaning (vectors); the small letter window adds neighbours (4 each side).
3. THINK, round 1: control vectors and memory vectors look at every letter; pass notes. NO call after round 1 (calls start after round 2 in the tested model).
4. THINK, round 2, then the call writer writes `sub 12 5`: show the copy trick (a learned pointer picks the number's last digit, copy steps one letter left until a learned stop-copy head says stop; 12 and 5 are copied, not typed).
5. CALCULATOR: plain Python returns 7; the line `sub 12 5 = 7` is read by the letter window as a new short string and added to the thinker's notes; the question is NOT re-read; the thinker keeps its vectors.
6. Round 3: thinker now sees the reply; call writer writes `add 7 3`; calculator returns 10; `add 7 3 = 10` joins the notes.
7. More rounds with "no call" (illustration: the project records no stop round for this question; label the stop round as an illustration).
8. The stop switch ("done?") is learned; at least 1 round, at most 32 (safety limit). One stop for the whole answer.
9. TALK: the talker copies `10` from the last reply; answer 10.
10. Legend scene: tested / built never tested / placeholder, exactly as the storyboard idea 1 scene 7 says: Gemma in front tested once (G1 3M seed 400); calculator-outside helper tested over 6 copies; calls-in-any-round and stop switch built never tested; English talker is a placeholder; never tested all together; open caveat: a few answers come with zero rounds.
