One task explained · sleep and old skills

Sleep Without Forgetting

After the model learns mazes, it forgets sums and grids. We look for cheap ways for sleep to bring them back.

Why it happens

New lessons overwrite old ones

The model has one set of dials. Turning them for mazes turns them away from sums.

What we already know

Sleep helps, but it needs a big notebook

At night the model re-practises old puzzles it kept. With 128 kept per kind it gets sums back to about 150 of 200. With 16 kept, it stays near zero.

Idea 1

Find where the forgetting lives

Undo the maze lesson in one part of the model at a time, then re-test sums, grids and mazes.

our best guess: it shows where to protect
Idea 2

Blend yesterday's model with today's

Mix the old weights and the new ones, like paint. Pick the mix using only the 16 kept puzzles. No training needed.

our best guess: about 1 chance in 3
Idea 3

Practise the weakest puzzles first

With few kept puzzles, spend the night on the ones the model now gets most wrong.

our best guess: about 1 chance in 3
What happens next

Cheap checks first, then the sleep change

Idea 1 and idea 2 only re-test saved models, so they cost nothing but computer time. Idea 3 changes sleep and comes last. Pass marks are written down before any run.
