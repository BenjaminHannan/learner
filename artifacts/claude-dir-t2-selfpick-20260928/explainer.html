Sleep test T2

Who picks what to remember?

After a hard new day the model forgets old skills. It can keep only 16 old puzzles per kind. What if it chooses the 16?

The problem

After a maze day it knows nothing old

Right answers on old sums: none. A sleep with 16 saved puzzles brings back only a few. 128 saved bring back many.

The test

Four ways to fill the 16 slots

One change only: which puzzles. Same sleep, same mazes, same steps.

How we judge

The bar is 3 times the wobble

Same sleep repeated 3 times moves by at most 4 (sums) and 5 (grids). So the gain must reach 12 and 16.

What would prove it wrong

Below +5 on both seeds

If the model's pick is no better than a random 16 on both kinds and both seeds, choosing does not help at this size. We guess that is 45 percent likely.
