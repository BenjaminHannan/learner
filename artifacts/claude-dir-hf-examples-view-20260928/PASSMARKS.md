# Pass marks: examples in view (fixed before any run)

Written 2026-09-28 21:3x UTC by Director helper HF, **before any run they judge**; no model has been built or scored, and none of these numbers comes from data. They are guesses set from the ruler's scale (`RESULTS-EQ.md`); the Director may change them **before** the first score exists, never after. Design: `DESIGN.md`. Counts are correct answers of 300 on a held-out kind's graded panel; each seed is judged alone, never pooled; each held-out kind is judged separately (at least 2).

## Validity (failure = INCONCLUSIVE for that kind; its holdout stays unopened)
- Item A has landed with at least 8 practised kinds, and H1's two kinds (or the A-designated held-out kinds) are held out of all practice.
- G1 to G4 of DESIGN section 4 all pass.
- U baseline counts at k = 1, 2, 4, 8 exist for that kind in both seeds from the same harness.

## Marks (per kind, per seed)
- **P1 examples help:** V at s = 8 is at least V0 + 30 of 300.
- **P2 examples are read, not just the format:** VS at s = 8 gains at most half of V's gain over V0 (VS - V0 <= (V - V0) / 2).
- **P3 beats updating at equal examples:** the mean of V over s = 1, 2, 4, 8 is at least the mean of U over k = 1, 2, 4, 8 + 15. (Weak bar: U is near 0 at k = 1 and 4 in the maze ruler. Reported next to P4.)
- **P4 headline:** V at s = 8 is at least 150 of 300. For reference, weight updates first reach 150 of 300 at k = 64 or 256 on mazes.
- **P5 beats plain:** V at s = 8 is at least Plain-V at s = 8 + 15.
- **P6 no harm:** G1 holds after episodic practice (already the guard) and V0 on each old kind is within 6 of 200 of the loop's original source score.

A kind PASSES if P1, P2, P4, P5, P6 hold in both seeds (P3 reported, not required). The design PASSES if at least 2 kinds pass. One kind passing gives "partial", as with the few-example ruler in the roadmap.

## The result that would prove it wrong
- V at s = 8 is below V0 + 10 of 300 in either seed on at least half of the held-out kinds: **examples in view do not work at this practice breadth** (rejected at about 10 kinds, this size, this budget; not a claim about other breadths).
- Or P2 fails while P1 holds (VS gains more than half of V's gain): the gain is from the format, so the claim "the net reads the examples" is false.
- Or P6 fails: gain came with forgetting; rejected.
- Reading a split: seed 0 pass and seed 1 fail = not promoted, not rejected.

## Report-only (no mark)
C3 (no episodic training), VU, order permutations, s = 16 if cost allows, the two-kinds-only check, mean rounds and cap hits, and V per rung.

## Blind recount
A step separate from the run recounts every count from the raw score files and this file only, before the verdict is written.
