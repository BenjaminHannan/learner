## Part A. Lead 3: does anything say "this answer is wrong"? (diagnostic, no gain claim, report-only)
Data: the read job's per-item records (DESIGN section 3), dev 9x9 mazes (300 per net), on the baseline loop's checkpoints k = 64, 256, 1,024, 4,096, 16,384 for seeds 0 and 1; sums and grids (200 each) on the same nets and the source net, as report-only rows.
- **Validity (else INVALID, nothing else claimed):** for every maze rung the recomputed `right`, `fixed_right`, `cap_hits` equal the baseline `adapt.json` values and `mean_rounds` agrees within 1e-9; for the source net the recomputed sums and grids `right` equal `source.json` `old`.
- **Eligible cell:** a (net, panel) with at least 10 wrong and at least 10 right of the panel. AUROC is only computed on eligible cells (a cell with 5 wrong of 300 cannot separate a signal from chance).
- **Noise (shown as a formula, numbers printed by the script):** the chance-level standard error of an AUROC with n_w wrong and n_r right is sqrt((n_w + n_r + 1) / (12 n_w n_r)); for 25 wrong of 300 that is 0.060, so the 0.70 bar is 3.3 SE above chance; the bar 0.60 is only 1.7 SE above chance. The script prints the SE for each cell.
- **A signal is USEFUL** when, in each seed, at least 3 maze rungs are eligible and AUROC >= 0.70 on at least 2/3 of the eligible rungs (rounded up). **NOT USEFUL** when AUROC <= 0.60 on **every** eligible maze rung in **both** seeds (the every-seed reading: one good seed or rung blocks a rejection). Otherwise **NOT SHOWN**.
- **Result that proves the idea wrong (the note's own wording):** if all five signals are NOT USEFUL, then nothing the net already computes flags its wrong maze answers, and Lead 2's "stable" target would only teach stopping on wrong answers. If at least one is USEFUL, "the net can tell when it is wrong" is shown for that signal on dev mazes only.
- **Coverage versus risk, report-only:** for each signal and eligible cell, error among the kept when the most confident 90, 75 and 50 percent are kept, beside the all-answer error, as "x of n". No mark; a reader should be able to see whether refusing 25 percent removes most of the errors.
- Not judged: sums and grids (report-only, see Eligible), 7x7 and 11x11, the holdout (unopened).

## Part B. Lead 1: stop when the answer is unchanged for N rounds, N in {3, 6} (ceiling, report-only, no product claim)
Same records. Per maze rung, per seed, per rule (ruler stop, fixed-16, unchanged-3, unchanged-6): `right`, `cap_hits`, `mean_rounds` of 300.
- A rung **passes the ceiling** for a rule when `right` >= `fixed_right` - 6 (the ruler's own stop-failure rule, `PROTOCOL.md:23`) and `cap_hits` <= 150 (at least half the mazes stop before the cap). A seed passes when at least 4 of the 5 judged rungs {64, 256, 1024, 4096, 16384} pass.
- Word per rule: **CEILING MET** when both seeds pass; **FAILS** when at most 2 of 5 rungs pass in **both** seeds; otherwise **NOT SHOWN**. Baseline reference (shown, DESIGN section 6): the ruler's stop passes the cap-hit half at 1 of 5 rungs in each seed (seed 0: k=16,384 only; seed 1: k=1,024 only), so it does not meet it.
- It is a hand-written rule, so a met ceiling is a target for Lead 2, not a build recommendation.

## Part C. Lead 2: label-free stop target in practice (the one change; diagnostic, report-only on accuracy)
**Variant:** loop, practice stop target = stable-now (DESIGN section 4), 12,000 source steps per seed (0 and 1), then the sealed equal-practice harness unedited (`--plugin claude_dir_sl_stop`, no plug-in Learner), `--init pre`, dev panels. **Control:** the baseline practised loop already on main (`eq-runs/loop-s{0,1}-pre/adapt.json`), same harness, same pool and batches, old practice target. The holdout is not opened. Not run: fresh loop, plain net (the plain net has no stop, so it cannot pass F1 by construction; it is not a control for a stop test).
- **V1 (source qualified):** for each new source net, sums4 and grids5 `right` >= 190 of 200 at the guard seed and `gradient_check.nonzero_all` true (`RESULTS-EQ.md:11-14`). A source failing V1 makes that seed INVALID-SOURCE: nothing below is claimed for it and it is reported.
- **V4 (same start as before the change):** `adapt.json` `optimizer_updates_per_rung` = 2,048, `weights` = 1,645,726, `persistent_coefficients` = 1,645,726, `lr` = 0.001, `support_sha256` equal to the baseline's for the same seed, `rung_batches` and `batch_size` equal to the baseline's. The source net is different (that is the change), so the cold rung 0 and old-kind counts are not compared for equality.
- **Judged rungs J** = {64, 256, 1024, 4096, 16384}; k = 1, 4, 16 reported only (the baseline nets solve at most 28 of 300 there). Fields from `adapt.json` `rungs[k]["9"]`: `right`, `fixed_right`, `mean_rounds`, `cap_hits`, n = 300.
- **F1 (the stop fires without maze labels):** a rung passes F1 when `right` >= 31 and `cap_hits` <= 150. A seed passes F1 when at least 4 of the 5 judged rungs pass. Baseline reference: 1 of 5 in each seed, so the baseline fails F1.
- **F2 (no stop failure):** a rung passes F2 when `right` >= `fixed_right` - 6 (the ruler's own rule). A seed passes F2 when all 5 judged rungs pass. Baseline: 5 of 5 in both seeds. A stop that fires at a fixed early round on every maze fails F2 unless the answer at that round is as good as at round 16, so F2 is the check that the stop is not just firing early.
- **Verdict words (seeds are judged alone, never pooled; the panel layouts are disjoint from every training layout, `RESULTS-EQ.md` "0 of 16,384 overlapping panel layouts", so a stop that fires cannot be memorising dev mazes):**
  1. **STOP FIRES WITHOUT LABELS:** F1 and F2 pass in **both** seeds.
  2. **FIRES BUT HURTS:** F1 passes in both seeds and F2 fails in at least one.
  3. **WRONG (the label-free target alone does not give a maze stop):** F1 fails in **both** seeds **and** at most 2 of 5 judged rungs pass F1 in both. This is the result that proves the idea wrong, whatever F2 says.
  4. **NOT SHOWN:** anything else, with each seed's rung list.
  An INVALID-SOURCE seed cannot support words 1, 2 or 3; with one seed valid the word is NOT SHOWN (at most).
- **Accuracy rows (report-only for promotion; they set the accuracy words; both required):** per seed Delta = variant minus control in percentage points on dev, for **F_eq** (mean of the 8 rungs of 100 x right / 300) and, as its own required row, **F_few** (mean of k = 1, 4, 16, 64). Bars **F_eq +7.0** and **F_few +8.5**, set by H12 from the baseline's seed-to-seed spread (shown recount: differences of `right` between the two baseline seeds at the eight rungs are 2, 0, 17, -47, -14, 42, -36, 25; RMS 28.2 counts over 8 rungs and 25.0 over the first 4; divided by sqrt(rungs) and by 3 gives SD 3.33 and 4.17 points; bar = 2 x SD rounded up). That spread already includes different practice runs (each seed has its own source net), so it is the right noise floor for a comparison that also changes the source net (suggested; two seeds only). Words: HELPS = Delta >= +bar in both seeds; HURTS = Delta <= -bar in both seeds; else NOT SEPARABLE FROM NOISE with both Deltas shown. The control value is the **higher of the baseline's learned-stop read and its fixed-16 read** for that seed (learned, in both seeds: 51.21 and 51.67 F_eq; 12.42 and 14.75 F_few); the variant value is its learned-stop read. Body reading (report-only): the same Deltas on the fixed-16 reads.
- **Recommendation rule:** only STOP FIRES WITHOUT LABELS with neither F_eq nor F_few HURTS supports recommending the stability target for practice. Adopting it in any build is Ben's and the Director's decision.
- Report-only after the verdict: all rungs' `right`, `fixed_right`, `mean_rounds`, `cap_hits` at 9x9, 7x7, 11x11; mean rounds by maze size at k >= 256; old-kind counts and sleep rows (no gates: single sleep draws move by about 14 of 200 on sums and 5 of 200 on grids with nothing changed, H8 REVIEW X1); side-by-side with H12's `adapt.json` if it has landed and is sealed (a column, not a comparator); the S1/S2 words of H12's PASSMARKS on the variant, for comparability.

5. **F_few beside F_eq.** F_few has its own bar (+8.5), its own word and is a required row (Part C).
6. **Sleep gates.** None used (Part C, report-only); any later gate must be the mean of 3 sleep draws for both arms with margin max(6, 2 x SE).
