# First three design race marks

Sealed 2026-09-27 22:36 UTC. Apply only after the baseline ruler passes V1–V3. Two paired seeds, the same panels and examples, and the same locked harness. All differences below are percentage points of the 9×9 holdout `F_all` score (mean of nine positive rungs), with each seed judged separately.

**Common gates for each design, in both seeds:** before maze adaptation, at least 95% on each old kind and within three points of the source-trained loop on sums and grids separately; `F_all` at least five points above the source-trained plain model and at least five points above the design's own fresh copy; after both 64-example and 64k sleep branches, each old kind within three points of the loop's corresponding old-kind score. All persistent coefficients count toward the 1.65M-weight budget (within 2% of the loop); raw example memory and inference rounds are disclosed. Learned stopping has the 48-round cap and the same source-selected fixed-depth check. Reaching 50% after 64 examples is report-only.

**Test A, feedback-written correction patches:** `F_all` at least 10 points above the loop in both seeds. The only architectural change is the feedback-written patch with its parameter reallocation. If it fits its supports but `F_all` is no higher than the loop in both seeds, or if it gains mazes only by breaking an old-kind gate, reject this implementation at this budget. If episodic learning-to-learn source training is used, give the loop control the same training in this race before comparing.

**Test B, hypothesis-gated dendritic branches:** `F_all` at least five points above the loop in both seeds, **and** mean old-kind forgetting `D` must be reduced by at least `max(3 points, half the loop's D)` on each paired seed and sleep branch. Here D is the mean of separately reported sums and grids losses. Post-sleep maze accuracy must be no more than three points below the paired loop on either branch. If the model preserves old skills by failing to learn mazes, or both seeds improve neither `F_all` nor D, reject the proposed learning-and-retention claim. If the loop scarcely forgets, report the retention endpoint INCONCLUSIVE for lack of headroom rather than changing replay.

**Test C, persistent relation state:** `F_all` at least 10 points above the loop in both seeds. If `F_all` is no higher than the loop in both seeds, reject the few-example advantage at this budget even if 11×11 after long training improves. A win for the whole circuit does not by itself isolate which component helped.

These are promotion thresholds, not predictions or claims of statistical certainty. The 7×7 and 11×11 panels, k=64 at 50%, fixed-depth/stop gap, compute, and raw-memory bytes are reported for each design. No change to marks after a design sees any dev or holdout maze score.
