## Build choices the design left open (each SHOWN in `scripts/claude_dir_g_run.py`; none is a mark)
1. Start-vector init N(0, 1.0) per entry (the loop's state is a LayerNorm output, unit scale; "small random" in DESIGN.md section 3 is read as "not a designed vector", and 1.0 keeps the four streams apart at round 1). `--start-std`. A much smaller init is the likeliest way to get INCONCLUSIVE-COLLAPSED (V4) by accident.
2. Gradient reaches the starts only on steps with no free (no-grad) round, about 15% of steps (total == k; SHOWN by `selftest`: no gradient with free rounds, gradient on all four without). The sealed detach after the free rounds is kept, so nothing but the starts and the loss weights changes. The starts still get plenty of movement in principle, but this is an UNTESTED reason why V4 could fail.
3. The four start vectors have weight decay 0 (the sealed 0.1 would pull them toward zero and toward each other); every other parameter keeps 0.1.
4. Per-item selection score = mean over the graded rounds of the item's mean token CE, detached; lowest wins, ties to the lowest stream index. Token loss = sum over items and streams of weight x summed token CE / total blank cells in the batch, averaged over graded rounds (so K = 1 is exactly the sealed loss).
5. `--serial-streams` (same gradient, about a quarter of the memory, more time) exists in case four streams at once do not fit the GPU; `selftest` shows parallel and serial agree (loss difference 4.8e-07, largest gradient difference 1.2e-07 of scale 0.66, CPU fp32).
6. Extra weights: 2,048 (4 x 512) on 6,438,302 = 0.032%, under the 0.05% in DESIGN.md.
