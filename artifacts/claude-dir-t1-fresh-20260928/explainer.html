Sleep test T1

Can made-up questions replace a saved pile?

Instead of saving old puzzles, make fresh ones each step and let the model's earlier self answer them.

Why try it

Saving 16 puzzles is too few

With 16 saved, every sleep recalls at most 26 of 200. Fresh puzzles never run out.

The change

Fresh puzzles, no true answers

The pre-maze model answers each fresh puzzle. The sleeping model copies those answers.

Fair comparison

Beat the best of three

Fresh must beat the top of: true-answer sleep, the earlier copy sleep, and copy-only with the saved 16.

How we judge

Gain 21 and 29, and reach 50

Gain must beat 3 times the wobble (21 sums, 29 grids). It must also reach 50 of 200, above anything a 16-pile ever got.

Honest limits

This is a stand-in

Code makes the puzzles, not the model. If this fails, we only learn fresh puzzles at this size do not help. Our guess: 5 percent pass.
