## What it means

Passing means the two paths wire51 never exercised now work: sleep installs a
word from a live log, and the installed word answers questions about new

## What it does not mean

It does not mean the real English ears can teach this word: FakeEars is a
stand-in, and the Qwen parser is known to reject 'maternal grandmother'.
A pass here exercises sleep + reasoning, not parsing.
