Labels: **shown** = measured, **suggested** = reasoned, **untested** = never run.

## Why (shown)

- L3: trained on one join layout (`K+J`), the 3M model does new kind pairs in that layout partly (LB 46.2, LA 15.5)
  and a new layout `(K)*(J)` not at all (LB 0.5, LA 0.9), while the practised layout reaches 94.5 / 76.4.
- L4 (halfway, 2,000 updates): practice from the swap tool with the join step (46% of its joined rows are top-level
  `K+J`, but mostly `cell+function` forms) leaves the test's `K+J` at 2.4 on both arms, though near rises to 45-68.
  So the model learns joins per exact form, not as a step it can reuse.
- Open question: is that because each run so far practised only one or a few layouts? Work on compositional learning
  (e.g. Lake & Baroni 2023) suggests training on many varied compositions is what makes a new one carry.
