## Why (shown)

- L1: given 6,000 generator-made joins (`K+J` and `(K)*(J)`, all kind pairs) the 3M model learns held-out joins of the
  same templates: rank-8 add-on (LA) 69.3, full fine-tune (LB) 88.8 after 4,000 updates (training slice 79.8 / 97.4).
- L2: given 6,000 joins made by the mode's own swap tool, same dose and arm LA, it learns those (training slice 42.1) but
  the test's joins stay at 2.1 (C1 proved wrong), while single factors rise (near 60.7, bigger grid 41.4, long range
  53.3, v3 near 63.8). The swap-made joins are dominated by a few templates from the one help example with `+`
  (`=R+PRODUCT(R:R)` 878, `=R+MAX(R:R)` 865, `=SUM(R:R)*n` 346, ...; 209 forms); the test's `(K)*(J)` form never occurs
  and the test's `K+J` forms occur 1-9 times each.
- So: does the model learn "join two results" as a skill that carries to new kind pairs and new arrangements, or only
  the templates it practised many times? The answer decides the next change.
