## In plain language

- **What was tested.** Premonition stores each fact line as a "memory card". A card is a small summary vector, built
  by pooling the words of that line. We tested two ways of building cards.
- **Averaged cards.** Averaging the words equally, instead of letting the model choose which words matter, made
  things worse:
  - reading one correct card fell from 512 to 363 of 512 (seed 0) and from 485 to 316 (seed 1);
  - picking the right card among look-alikes did not improve.
- **Separate address and content.** Giving each card separate address and content summaries did not help either.
  - Under the default training recipe, the retrieval model's answers ignore the cards completely, whichever card
    type it uses.
  - With separate summaries, it fetched both needed cards less often on practised two-step questions.
- **The new training objective (H1).** H1 teaches the model to keep its answer when an irrelevant fact changes, and
  to change its answer when the relevant fact changes.
  - It is built and tested, but it was **not trained**.
  - The rule we fixed in advance says H1 may only be tried once the model can already read and choose cards better
    than a guessing shortcut. It cannot yet: choosing is about 0.32, below the 0.537 shortcut.
- **Why that matters.** Almost no model gets both halves of a "changed-fact" pair right (0–2 of 256). The model is
  not yet linking questions to the right facts.
- **The next repair.** Answers should read the right card's words more directly, before any objective like H1.

## What was built (all additive; no existing file edited)

**`premonition/ladder_triplets.py`: simulator-verified triplets.**
- Each triplet has three versions of one world:
  - x, the original;
  - u, an irrelevant change: a decoy swap or a decoy value;
  - v, a relevant change: a subject swap or a relation swap. Both swaps keep the same set of words.
- An independent oracle checks every answer.
- The edits touch only the intended fact lines.
- Roles, change kinds and answers are kept in loss-side metadata only. They never enter the batch; the row order is
  shuffled and question ids are neutral.
- Training triplets contain no held-out two-hop question.

**`premonition/paired_objectives.py`: H1 (`H1Config`, off by default).**
- The loss is the ordinary CE plus λ·JS(p_x, p_u) plus μ·max(0, m − Δ), with Δ the changed-answer log-odds shift.
- Numerics: float32 and log space; non-finite logits are refused.
- The single-token-answer diagnostic is the first answer position.
- The both-correct metrics mean a constant answer cannot pass.
- H1 cannot train the detached top-k selection or ASK. This is documented and tested.

**`scripts/premonition_pool_controls.py`: the milestone harness.**
- Imports: a frozen, byte-identical source snapshot.
- Matching across writers: shared initialisation and RNG stream, and the same data order.
- Retrieval arms: all use the original writer's calibrated FLOP budget and schedule, so every arm stops at step 1517
  with identical phases.
- Evaluation: read-only.
- Test split: scored once per predeclared checkpoint.
- Statistics: visit- or triplet-clustered one-sided 99% bootstrap.
- Ledger: every charge is recorded, including failures.

**Tests:**
- `tests/test_premonition_paired_objectives.py`;
- `tests/test_premonition_pool_controls.py`.

They cover:
- JS against a float64 reference;
- zero gradient at agreement;
- finiteness at logits of 1e30;
- the direction of the margin gradient;
- masking;
- CE parity when H1 is off;
- oracle, inventory and minimal-edit checks;
- no role metadata in the batch;
- identical writer initialisations;
- key/value gradient separation;
- the answer loss never reaching the separate key pool;
- checkpoint round-trip;
- schedule identity;
- the bootstrap.

**Scope limits honoured.**
- No decoder key exposure, no new controller.
- No H2, TST, sleep or fast-weight memory.

## Limits

- **Seeds.** Retrieval is seed 0 only; answer-only uses 2 seeds. Both are screens: finalists need 3 seeds.
- **Setting.** A toy ladder with synthetic vocabulary. Supplied and evidence cards are privileged diagnostics, not
  primary evidence.
- **H1 weights.** λ = μ = 1 and m = 1 nat are untuned, and were never trained.
- **Test split.** The ladder test split was consulted overnight for other checkpoints, so it is not untouched. The
  triplet test set is fresh.
- **Card-blindness scope.** It holds under D's default curriculum only. The evidence audit notes that long-read
  checkpoints behave differently; they were not rerun here.
- **Probes.** No probes were used. Low choosing does not prove that the cards lack person or relation information.
- **Two-pool scope.** It only changes retrieval keys, which the decoder never sees, so it cannot create a new answer
  pathway.

## One recommendation

**Repair choosing before any H1 or H2 work.**
- Run the direct contextual-state reader diagnostic from `03-learning.md`:
  - hold the selected card lines fixed;
  - have the decoder read those lines' original contextual token states from the same reader pass, versus the
    pooled card.
- Use the answer-only choosing setup with matched controls and at least 2 seeds.
- Re-apply this milestone's unchanged gate. H1 stays built and tested, ready if that gate passes.
