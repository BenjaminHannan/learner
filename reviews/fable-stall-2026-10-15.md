# Outside-opinion brief for Fable 5.1 (xhigh, Message Batches): the self-imitation loop stalled; what next? (written 2:54 PM ET 10-15)

Stand-alone; it is only about the 3M game-world test (Crafter as text), not the card experiments or the village model.

I run a small research project. A 3-million-parameter language model plays Crafter as text: each step it reads a 9x7 letter map (trees T, water ~, grass ., stone o, table t, cows C, zombies Z, the player as ^ v < >) plus health, food, drink, energy and what it carries, and answers with one of 17 move words (left right up down do sleep table wpick ...), chosen by softmax over summed letter log-probs. Only a rank-8 add-on (135,488 numbers) trains; the base is frozen. Score = mean distinct achievements ("unlocks") a game on 100 held-out maps; random play 2.31. Rules: everything the finished model does must be learned (a hand-written teacher or labeller may exist only in the test harness to measure things, never in the shipped model); no new teacher model; the game's own reward is +1 on the first unlock of each achievement and health/10; the real targets (Minecraft, a spreadsheet) cannot be copied, so anything that copies the world is an instrument, not deployable; free CPU only (4 cores, about 60 moves a second, 10 s per 256-view update), runs up to about 10 h.

## What we know (shown = measured; two seeds, 200 and 201)
1. Every reward-only way of learning plateaus at 3.3 to 4.8 unlocks: 8-move hindsight copy 3.3 to 3.5; learned value guesser 1.8 / 3.2; PPO-clipped night on the add-on 4.15 / 4.19 (about half of its gain over the untrained model is a map-blind move habit: a fixed habit with the same move odds scores 3.96 / 3.87); a small PPO network 3.7 to 4.4; a 24-move look-ahead search through copies of the world playing alone 4.75 (it grabs sapling, plant, wake_up, wood in 69% of games, table 28%, pickaxe 3%, no stone). Untrained model 3.75 / 3.61.
2. Capacity is not the wall: a hand-written labeller (harness only; plays 8.23 itself) labels views; training the same add-on by cross entropy on 64,000 such labels gives 6.50 / 7.10 and a wood pickaxe on 83% / 91% of maps, stone 40% / 43% (16,000 labels: 4.88 / 4.62; below 4,000 no better than a fixed word). The wall is the chain: walk to a tree, collect wood, place a table (2 wood), make a wood pickaxe (1 wood, table within one cell), walk to stone, mine it (needs the pickaxe). Every link pays its own +1, but each needs the one before it, so the later links almost never occur in the model's own play.
3. Your earlier answer (to a previous brief) was: iterated fastest-segment self-imitation: play 400 games, cut each game at every achievement-counter rise (repeats count), keep per event type the fastest 30% of segments (at most 12 moves), weight by rarity (n^-1/2, at most 20:1), keep a replay buffer, train 3 epochs (about 90 updates) on new rows plus as many buffer rows, repeat; marks fixed in advance (leading indicator: pickaxe games must triple by round 8; stone above 10 per 400 games by round 16; final sealed screen: both seeds at least 5.2 and pickaxe on 25% of maps).
