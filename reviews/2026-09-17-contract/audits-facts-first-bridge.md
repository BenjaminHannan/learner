STRONGEST ATTACK: The method-learning bridge (Episode C), which the recommendation leans on to justify facts-first ("The bridge from A to C is strong"), has a split that cannot exist in the world it defines. But the world's own typing (lives_in person->town, works_for person->guild, based_in guild->town, mentor_of/landlord_of person->person, rival_of guild->guild; towns have no outgoing relation) makes a 2-chain "r2 of r1 of X" type-valid only when r1 outputs a person or guild: r1 in {mentor_of, landlord_of} admits at most 6 second relations each, r1 in {works_for, rival_of} at most 2-3 each, for roughly 16 type-valid ordered pairs in total. So "16 held-out compositions" is either every valid program (leaving nothing to meta-train the interpreter on) or is padded with unanswerable chains like "the town of the town of X". Either way the stated task-identification control ("Ruled out by the held-out pairs") does not exist as written, and with a program space of ~16 the cheapest passing strategy for C is a 16-way classifier over definition sentences feeding a fixed 2-hop routine, which is parameter selection of a known operation, the exact confound the brief names. This hits Episode C Q1-Q3 and the "smallest observation that would change the recommendation" clause, which is conditioned on C passing at B's budget. A secondary milestone-one attack: W is reset tabula rasa per episode (Section 2.2 "initialized tabula rasa each episode"; Section 4.3 swap treats each test episode as its own W), so the evaluator hands the model an implicit task-boundary signal the brief forbids, and "persistence" is demonstrated only across one restart inside a 32-sentence episode, never across episodes.

- Supervision contracts are not kept distinct in the practice phase: the brief requires pass/fail, answer-revealing correction, demonstrations and instructions to be labeled separately; Episode D's replay-on-fail is answer-revealing correction routed through the teaching channel but is tabled as 'pass/fail only'.
- For two-hop practice queries ('a fixed symbolic chain-checker resolves the chain over L'), the memo does not say which logged string is replayed on failure (both hops? the one whose single-hop practice also fails?), so the practice write policy is underspecified exactly where a chain repair matters.

- The rollback intervention is offered as the test of rank-1 locality, but its expected outcome is stated without the key-overlap dependence; the brief's distinction 'orthogonal keys help one-pass writes; they are not necessary for a linear solution to exist' implies the rollback expectation must be conditioned on measured key overlaps or replaced by a counterfactual re-run without teaching j.
- The evaluation budget is not reconciled with the ten-minute cap: 5 seeds x 200 episodes x (2x2 cells + 4 baselines + 5 interventions + template splits) is not costed, and the six-chained-run meta-training budget is asserted for the learned writer with no fallback timing for the fixed-rule writer.

VERDICT: I would adopt this as milestone one, with the discriminator re-founded. The randomized-world, teach-then-clear-then-query design, the intervention set, the byte-matched baselines, the signed damage accounting and the explicit log boundary are the most careful evidentiary scaffolding on the panel, and its citations are almost all correctly attributed to the digest. But the memo's own headline, that correction-then-two-hop 'cannot' be passed by a cache and 'can' be passed by workspace-conditioned retrieval, is asserted rather than derived, and it depends on an unstated one-rank-1-write-per-sentence invariant while the outer loop trains the writer on exactly those queries; I would state that invariant as a contract term and make the 2x2 plus the ||m_{t+1} - m_t|| diagnostic the primary result, with the two-hop score as a component. I would fix the Episode C composition split (only 12 type-valid chains exist, not 64) before presenting it as a bridge, relabel practice-phase replay as answer-revealing correction, and add to the 'not supported' list that persistence is intra-episode plus restart with a tabula-rasa W, not accumulation over a lifetime. The bridge to method learning is honestly rated as narrow; after the composition fix it is narrower still, and the memo should say that Contract C, as specified, tests reading a stored (r1, r2) pair from a handful of held-out chains rather than anything a reader would call a program.
