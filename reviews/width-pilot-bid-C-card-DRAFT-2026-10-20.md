## 2b. R1 = A: conditions before seal (coordinator 10-20, round 2)

**New parents (stated, coordinator 10-20):** a C2b parent cannot load under the Bid C caps, so **Bid C trains NEW parents under the caps** at both widths. "Same family as the C2b parents" means the **architecture only** (ledger, copy=True, 2 blocks, 8 loops, reader 2), not the C2b checkpoints.

**Load gate (ACCEPTED in this three-check form, coordinator 10-20; before any arm; on a fail: stop and report, no patch-and-retry).**

The coordinator's wording ("load a C2b parent with caps applied first and reproduce its score") cannot pass as written. Option A's caps set `reader.N_PLACE = 37`, while a C2b parent's place table has 16 rows (`nn.Embedding(N_PLACE, d)`, `custom_io/models/reader.py:72`), so the state dict cannot load. The gate is therefore split into three checks, all **bit-identical on CPU** (same exact-match count and the same per-row answers on a fixed batch; float outputs compared with `torch.equal`), with no tolerance:
1. **Caps plumbing check.** Apply a caps file equal to the old defaults (`max_ans 8`, `n_reg 9`, so `N_PLACE 16`) **before** `load_parent`, load C2b parent s200, and reproduce its recorded per-row C2b-test score bit-identically on a fixed 64-row batch. This shows the caps-first load path is correct and inert at the defaults.
2. **Negative check.** Applying the Bid C caps (`n_reg 36`) and then loading a C2b parent must **fail with a shape error**. If it loads, caps were not applied before load: stop.
3. **Fixed-caps round trip.** The calibration base parent P_0, trained under the Bid C caps, is reloaded in a fresh process with caps applied first. It must reproduce its own end-of-training eval on a fixed batch bit-identically on CPU.
- **Runtime assert:** every c2b train and score entry point asserts, before `load_parent`, that `caps.apply` has run with the sealed caps file (its sha256 is recorded in each run's output).

**Inert count (measured 10-20, CPU, from `creative/data/c2/*.jsonl` on `claude/project-thread-2zeaoc`):**

- **Zero C2 rows exceed the old caps.** On the puzzle rows themselves, the overlay is needed only for loading and has no direct effect on their targets or scores. C2c is drawn by the same generator, so the same is expected; C2c's own count is checked at generation.

## 3. Readout and pre-registered marks (house form)

**Unit:** the seed-index i (paired parents P_i at both widths). Every statistic is over seed-indices, never over questions or replicates (house rule; `custom_io/pilot_bars.py`, review head a2a83f9f0).

**Headroom kinds (fixed now, from the C2b record):** kinds where base W first-try was < 50%: **affine (1.5%), double_add (16.7%), sq_plus (14.4%)**. Square (94.7%) and last_digit (69.1%) are near ceiling for search, so they are report-only.

**Primary contrast:** d_i = W first-try on the headroom kinds (points)_wide − (same)_base.
**Co-headline (report):** pooled (W−N′)_wide − (W−N′)_base.

**S** = max(sigma_d measured at the gate, floor 3.0). The floor is the C2b between-parent sd of W−N, 3.15 rounded down. It is a floor, not a bar.

| Verdict | Condition (all must hold) |
|---|---|
| **SIZE (width helps)** | mean d ≥ max(10, 2·S) **and** d_i > 0 on every seed-index (worst-seed check) **and** no headroom kind's mean d < −2 (per-kind check) **and** the guards below hold |
| **NOT SIZE (width ruled out at this power)** | mean d < max(5, S) **after the horizon extension**: a KILL is horizon-locked (§4) |
| **UNRESOLVED (designed)** | sigma_d > 6 at the cap of n = 10, or neither verdict reached. The tripwire is 6 and not v2's 4 because C2b's single-size spread already implies sigma_d ≥ about 4.5 if parents don't correlate across widths (suggested). |
| **GUARD FAIL (overrides)** | skills harm > 2 points on any parent at either width (N′ minus W, pooled-5 chain families; the live harm guard), **or** the wide practised check (256 fresh add/mult, reach@32) more than 2 points below base |

- **Seed buying:** tranches of 2 seed-indices until SE(d) ≤ 2.0, cap n = 10.
- **Printed beside every result:** the model-free blind baseline per kind (right at 1k / 6k / 50k candidates), N′ per width, and the by-kind table.
- **The result that would prove the size framing wrong:** after the extension, mean d < max(5, S) with SE ≤ 2.0. Width at 2.21× then does not move headroom-kind first-try by even max(5, S) points.
- **Also wrong:** if base N′ already scores within 5 points of base W on the headroom kinds (the sleep, not size, is spent), the contrast is uninformative and is reported as such.
