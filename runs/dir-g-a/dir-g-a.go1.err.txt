## Build choices the design left open (each SHOWN in `scripts/claude_dir_g_run.py`; none is a mark)
1. 5. `--serial-streams` (same gradient, about a quarter of the memory, more time) exists in case four streams at once do not fit the GPU; `selftest` shows parallel and serial agree (loss difference 4.8e-07, largest gradient difference 1.2e-07 of scale 0.66, CPU fp32).

    def __init__(self, K=K_DEFAULT, start_std=START_STD, zero_start=False):
        super().__init__("loop")
        self.K = K
        d = R.ARMS["loop"]["d"]

        self.starts = nn.Parameter(init)

        """start states for the streams in ks, stream-major rows [len(ks) * B, T, d]"""
