## Read, not judged
- **emb beats ptr** by +30.6 on unseen (CI +15.3 to +45.8; per seed 25, 26, 39, 42, 45, 6) and +31.1 on all questions.
- **Thin-talker check (suggested, not proven):** zeroing the core's 8 pooled vectors at test drops ptr to 2.4% and
  emb to 0.0%. So in neither arm can the frozen LM answer from the raw words alone. Caveat: zeroing is a crude lesion;
  the LM may simply be thrown by an all-zero prefix. A fairer test is a shuffled-core lesion (core vectors from
  another story), untested.
- ptr is still weak on new wording (37.6% vs 77.0%) and underfit (train fit 74.7%); emb fits everything.

## Caveats

  letting it read the passage; it is the stronger exit, but it makes the talker less thin. Which to keep is Ben's call.
