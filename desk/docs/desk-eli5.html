Premonition Desk

Your own helper app on the Mac: you type a job, it does the clicking.

picture is an example job

It has its own web browser

It gives every button a number, then says "click 7", the way you'd point.

It can use your Mac apps too

A small helper reads each app's buttons and moves the mouse and keys for it.

Far less to read before every chat

OpenBot sends a huge setup note each time; this app sends a short one, so first replies come much sooner.

Desk number is our estimate from 4,374 letters

You stay the boss

Pick "Ask first" or "Hands-off"; it always asks before buying, sending or deleting, and Stop works any time.

Two switches you flip once

System Settings → Privacy & Security: turn on Premonition Desk under both of these.

macOS asks again after each rebuild

What happens next

It's installed on the M3; it starts working once the M3's model server is back on.
