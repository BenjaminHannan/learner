How a picture reaches the reasoner

Design v2, 3 Oct. Checked on CPU with the real frozen image encoder and made-up pictures. Nothing has been trained on a GPU yet.

The path

The workspace is the shared format for text, images and sound. Each piece says what it is, where it sits in the picture, and when it happened.

Does shrinking 16x16 to 8x8 lose anything?

Linear probe accuracy (%) on 500 test pictures per task. 8x8 matched 16x16 everywhere, including a row of nine tiny digits like a Minecraft hotbar (99.8%). So the plan keeps 8x8.

Pass marks were written before running. Caveat: these are clean made-up pictures. Real Minecraft screenshots, with its pixel font and textures, are the next check.

Roadmap to playing Minecraft from the screen

The eye and reasoner run 10 to 20 times a second. The big language model stays out of that fast loop and is only called to talk or read guides. OpenAI's VPT and DreamerV3 reached diamonds with thousands of hours of video or nine GPU-days, so we borrow their ideas at small scale. All later steps are untested.
