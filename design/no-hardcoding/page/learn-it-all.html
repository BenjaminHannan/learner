1 · What runs today

Python cuts the numbers out first

17 hand-written parts run every time B2 answers; the model only learns which step to take and which number to point at.

2 · Where it's going

The model writes, a tool calculates

Numbers stay as letters: the model copies them into a call, and it reads the calculator's reply in its next round.

Remove the hand parts in two groups

Two steps are done and group 1 is built; group 2 removes the last hand parts, and none ever goes back in.

4 · Your second question

Letters keep every bit of the question

Word pieces pack about 4 letters per slot, but they hide spelling and cut numbers at odd places.

5 · What our tests showed

Gemma's meaning helps; keep the 9-letter window

Gemma's meanings added about 2 points here and 2.7 on average over 6 more runs; every reader without the 9-letter window broke the cipher puzzles.

caveat · it answered a few questions with no thinking (6.6 vs a 4.6 limit)
