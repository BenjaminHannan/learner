## The stop

```
C0 STOP: c0: the S0 train-overhead probe did not complete (status 'aborted': peak reserved GPU memory 14.43 GiB
exceeds the 14.00 GiB limit; ...); S0 would abort the same way
```

The train-overhead probe is a short (20 s) real `train_d` of S0, run inside the C0 process. It covers the start-up
caches, FLOP calibration, the training steps, the final validation and the save. It reached **14.43 GiB reserved**,
5 GiB above what the bench's S0m4 training steps reached on the same stream (9.63 GiB).

**Suggested, not determined** (the abort message gives no phase, and the probe ran inside the C0 process):
1. **train_d's final validation.** It groups validation visits by the training `max_tokens` (55,000), not the eval's
   16,384, and S0's answer path holds Q × M desk rows. A forward pass that large may need more than a training step.
2. **The FLOP calibration** inside train_d.
3. **Live allocations left over in the C0 process** from the bench and probes (the peak counter was reset before the
   probe, but live tensors count). In that case the real trains, each a fresh process, would not trip.

Only (3) would make the stop an artefact of C0. (1) or (2) mean the registered S0 train would abort at its end, after
paying for its full T. The C0 stop exists to prevent exactly that.
