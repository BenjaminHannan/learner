## Six methods examined in depth

**1. Counterfactual language data and paired supervision.** [Kaushik, Hovy and Lipton, *Learning the Difference That Makes a Difference*](https://arxiv.org/html/1909.12434), §§3–5, minimally edit sentiment/NLI examples to change labels, then train on original-plus-edited data. This is direct language precedent for H1's ordinary augmentation competitor; it does not add H1's paired JS/margin loss. Some original-distribution accuracy can be sacrificed, and augmentation success does not establish procedural transfer. [Teney et al., *Learning What Makes a Difference from Counterfactual Examples and Gradient Supervision*](https://arxiv.org/html/2004.09034), §§3.2,4.3 and Appendix C, additionally align input gradients with counterfactual differences using cosine loss. Their language experiments compare against the same augmented data; simple bag-of-words models and small datasets limit transfer. They report two extra backpropagations per batch, and ordinary augmentation hurts their NLI results. This is closer objective prior art than medical imaging. [CF-SimCLR](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.09605v2) remains only a distant matched-data-control precedent: its SimCLR+ receives the same generated images without explicit pairing.

**2. Good-Enough Compositional Data Augmentation, GECA.** It substitutes fragments that occurred in shared environments, including discontinuous fragments, creating input/output examples without altering the model. Its SCAN experiment depends on a few bridging primitive examples; removing those prevents synthesis. Thus coverage of interchangeable roles matters more than a generic easy-to-hard ordering. GECA changes training support: synthesizing the held-out second-hop relation would invalidate our existing exclusion, even without copying test rows. Simulator verification can reject invalid compositions, but cannot make leaked composition support legitimate. Expanded data, generation, and training exposure must be charged. [Primary paper, §§3–4](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.676.pdf).

**3. Grokked Transformers are Implicit Reasoners.** Its synthetic composition study finds facts usable alone but unavailable in the upper-layer second-hop circuit. Parameter sharing allows some out-of-distribution transfer, while ordinary extended training does not establish systematic composition. This closely resembles our relation-position problem, but the paper uses parametric fact storage and a different architecture. Premonition already shares its recurrent block; sharing alone is not a fresh prescription. The useful lesson is to test whether one lookup procedure is reused across positions. Longer training incurs substantial cost without guaranteeing transfer. This paper was already identified overnight. [Primary paper, §3 and Appendix E.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.15071v2).

**4. Dark Experience Replay, DER/DER++.** A reservoir stores examples and historical logits; squared logit matching resists behavioral drift. DER++ adds replay cross-entropy against true labels, partly addressing misleading logits around distribution changes. This is functional preservation without a live teacher. Its experiments provide serious contrary evidence to projection enthusiasm: gradient-based competitors can be weaker, and ordinary replay remains strong in some settings. Generative outputs make storage grow with answer length and vocabulary; old distributions can preserve shortcuts. This is a fair later competitor, not evidence that Premonition already forgets. [Primary paper, §§3–5](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2020/file/b704ea2c39778f07c617f6b7ce480e9e-Paper.pdf).

**5. Continual backpropagation.** Mature, low-utility units are replaced; incoming weights are reinitialized and outgoing weights zeroed. Utility, activation, and age statistics add state; Adam moments and per-weight ages need reset handling. The paper studies prolonged loss of learning ability, not just forgetting old answers. Its heuristic utility estimate is not global functional importance. The project's pooling specialization and short seed-sensitive runs do not establish this failure mode. Unit replacement is already discussed locally, so renaming it would add nothing. [Primary paper, §6 and Appendix E](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.13812v3).

**6. Self-Distillation Fine-Tuning, SDFT, including August 2026 revision.** A demonstration-conditioned EMA teacher supervises student-generated trajectories. Although theory motivates reverse KL, the revision reports forward KL working best in practice. Weak in-context teaching is a material limitation: its 3B model underperforms SFT. It reports roughly 2.5× FLOPs and 4× wall time versus SFT in its setting, plus possible inherited textual artifacts. These are not projected Premonition costs. Already-planned card-in-hand tutoring is therefore neither new nor the next repair; a correct final answer also cannot certify every intermediate soft target. [Primary paper, §§3–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.19897v2).

The broader sweep also checked [A-GEM](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1812.00420): gradient projection adds computation and only protects sampled average behavior; without measured forgetting it is not a current priority. [2026 curriculum theory](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.27721v1) assumes deterministic Markovian structure and intermediate-state access or short-block coverage, not our full benchmark. [On-Policy Replay](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.29495v1) can preserve confident errors. [Counterfactual Simulation Training](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.20710v1) uses an LLM behavior monitor, not an exact world interpreter. None justifies teacher inference now.

## H1 — Teach which changes matter with verified paired objectives

**Plain explanation.** Changing somebody else's shoe colour should not change the answer about Mira's friend. Changing the friend's actual shoe colour should. Teach both rules together.

**Mechanism.** For a fixed question, construct a valid world `x`, an irrelevant-change sibling `u` with the same answer `a`, and a relevant-change sibling `v` with different answer `b`. Include verified value swaps between queried and decoy entities: identical word inventories then require different bindings. Keep wording, entity introductions, and answer frequency balanced; regenerate the complete world after intervention and verify all targets. Merely lacking an evidence tag does not establish irrelevance. Current `scheduler.counterfactual_pair` maximizes answer-changing links; `_twin_links` explicitly excludes equal answers. Add a separately versioned invariant-pair dataset, not an assumption that existing twins provide it.

Begin with single-token answers in a declared toy diagnostic. Keep ordinary cross-entropy on all three examples. Add `λ JS(p_x,p_u)` and a changed-pair term `μ max(0,m−Δ)`, where `Δ=log[p_x(a)/p_x(b)]−log[p_v(a)/p_v(b)]`. Here distributions concern the same question's answer, not the entire card representation. Keep other losses fixed; choose coefficients and margin only on development data under any future authorized tuning allowance.

**Prior art and novelty.** H1 combines established counterfactual augmentation, consistency and ranking ideas; its exact project transfer is untested. Kaushik supplies the direct language augmentation precedent and Teney the paired-supervision precedent. Neither checked paper uses this same explicit invariant-pair JS plus changed-pair answer-margin objective. Teney's gradient alignment has a closely related sensitivity aim and, geometrically, suppresses orthogonal sensitivity locally; it does not enforce H1's finite invariant-pair agreement. This limited two-paper check establishes no worldwide novelty. The new-to-project test targets decoy/subject binding. Hard own-ASK selection blocks direct answer gradients into query/key scoring; choosing gains must not be called repaired next-query reuse.

**Costs and fair test.** Compare shuffled ordinary augmentation, grouped triplets with cross-entropy only, and grouped triplets plus these losses. Every arm sees the exact same examples and targets. Last two share minibatches, separating objective from grouping. There is no teacher or inference module; extra state is pair IDs and temporary answer distributions. Charge generation, verification, padding, loss computation, and fewer steps under equal total compute; also report equal-example checkpoints. A vocabulary JS term costs work even when logits already exist.

**Decisive outcome and failure.** Require better choosing, both-correct changed pairs, and correct invariant pairs on fresh bindings. Keep question wording fixed: this tests binding, not paraphrase robustness. Separately test the excluded relation's two-hop use; choosing gains alone establish no composition claim. Constant predictions cannot pass both-correct metrics. Reject the objective if ordinary augmentation/grouping explains the gain or relevant sensitivity falls. Cross-entropy may already suffice; invariant regularization may reward ignorance. This is the first recommended learning screen, conditional on a usable identity/value path and shallow solvability.

## H2 — Teach a second lookup to reuse the standalone lookup

**Plain explanation.** Once Mira's friend is identified as Oren, “Mira's friend's colour” requires the same search as “Oren's colour.” Teach the existing query maker that these searches should agree.

**Mechanism.** Pair a permitted chain `colour(friend(Mira))` with suffix `colour(Oren)` in independent forks of the same label-free fact prefix. Both receive ordinary answer/retrieval training. Capture student scores on the next Think after an actual own-selected, positive-ASK correct-link insertion, before answer-card insertion; another retrieval opportunity must remain. Gold-preloaded/teacher-forced cases are ineligible. Add `λ KL(stopgrad(p_standalone) || p_chain_second)` over the chain's current eligible cards, excluding every previously fetched real card and retaining NULL. Mask before softmax, align source identities, and require teacher rank correctness on that actual distilled support. Full timing and leakage checks appear below. This directly trains scores before detached top-k, not ASK/HALT decisions.

**Privileges and split discipline.** Simulator bridge pairing and evidence-based eligibility are extra structural supervision: this is explicitly a **privileged diagnostic**, not answer-only learning or primary evidence. Controls receive identical pairs and evidence labels. No oracle bridge enters the chain's prefix or inference state. Exclude A2(F(x)) chains and bridge-conditioned auxiliary pairs from the augmented manifest; ordinary standalone A2 questions and A2 facts/negative cards remain allowed. Historical A2 two-hop tests have already informed this research: newly frozen worlds are fresh instances of a known composition shift, not an untouched composition family.

**Evidence, prior art, novelty.** The 9/171 correct-relation and 2/171 correct-person second fetches motivate this intervention. Self-distillation/consistency and Wang's positional specialization are closest precedents. The specific cross-position score objective is new to the inspected project; worldwide novelty is unproved. It differs from the already proposed explicit pointer-plus-relation query construction. One-hop competence supplies a conditional teaching signal, not a guarantee that its soft distribution is useful.

**Costs and decisive comparison.** Compare shuffled augmentation, grouped pairs with unchanged losses, grouped pairs plus KL, and the critical control: **same pairs/gating with extra ordinary second-fetch evidence cross-entropy**. Existing labels already identify the remaining answer card after a correct link; stronger supervision may explain any gain. Match added loss opportunities and tuning allowances, data, loops, initialization and measured total compute. Reuse the same paired scores in every arm or charge all extra passes. State adds pair IDs and temporary distributions, with no EMA teacher or inference overhead.

Report eligibility frequency, first-hop accuracy, second-fetch person/relation rank, actual fetches, halting, both-facts recall, and unfiltered end-to-end composition. Failure is improvement only on practiced pairs, lower divergence without better retrieval, or no advantage over augmentation/extra evidence CE. Scarce eligible examples make the screen inconclusive. Soft targets can add wrong-card structure. Recommend this only as a separate diagnostic after standalone retrieval/readout and choosing work, with the direct repeated reader/query first. Retention remains a later check with identical uniform replay, not another proposal.

## Evaluation contract

First establish shallow solvability under the repaired, frozen representation; do not combine architectural repairs with H1/H2. Ordinary question-centred answer supervision is already planned and must be the strong baseline. Preserve all frozen composition exclusions; curricula may rearrange permissible examples but may not introduce held-out primitives, relation positions, or wording. Separate new names, wording, depth, composition, and genuinely new procedures. Proof size is not sequential depth.

Use the versioned label-free path before binding names, exact tokenizer/preprocessing/checkpoint identities, and read-only evaluation. Oracle evidence, traces, and supplied cards stay explicitly privileged diagnostics with matched supervision, never primary verdict evidence. Generated answer targets are supervision, not visible earlier-answer inputs. Renaming requires equivariant output remapping, not indiscriminate invariance.

Finalists require three precommitted seeds, every run reported, validation-only development, and untouched test instances. Do not describe the already observed A2 shift as an untouched composition-selection test. Bootstrap paired results by originating visit, keeping all siblings together; respect the 60-visit/100-item floors and existing 99% bounds. Report preparation, failed trials, re-encoding, optimizer/replay state, FLOPs, and same-device wall time. Maintain `full_verdict: false` until the full contract is implemented. No training allowance remains implied by old reports or these proposed screens.

## Focused cross-critique: H2 validity and the proposed join

Read [01-reasoning.md](/Users/ben-hannan/Desktop/projects/beautiful-model/design/research/final-sweep-2026-09-19/01-reasoning.md) and inspected `CardStore.eligible/ask` and the query loop. These are proposed checks, not executed experiments.

**H1's gradient boundary.** `model.py` selects own cards with `store.top(scores.detach(), ...)`. H1 can train answer-visible values, readout and shared representations, but has no direct derivative through that choice into query/key scores or the binary ASK decision. Indirect shared-feature effects are possible; they are not evidence of learned query reuse. H2 trains scores before this detach; its KL does not directly train ASK/HALT. Report rank, actual fetch, halt and full-answer performance separately.

**H2 prefix and support, corrected by Hubble's final audit.** Fork independently from the same label-free **fact** prefix, pointer mapping, source identities and question-line age reference. Never place the oracle-derived suffix before the chain: the causal reader could leak its bridge through question history, even without a question card. Do not sequence either question after the other or share suffix-conditioned states. Reuse only common-prefix computation. Let E be the chain's currently eligible real cards before historical-fetch exclusion and F **all** previously fetched real cards, including subsequently evicted ones. Use `M=(E−F)∪{NULL}` for both distributions, masking scores before softmax and renormalizing on valid coordinates. Align stable source-line identities rather than assuming batch indices match. Gate teacher correctness on the **actual distilled support**; report native standalone competence separately. A shadow mask must not modify the teacher's ordinary standalone training episode. This supersedes the earlier native-support gating requirement.

**H2 event capture.** The student distribution must come from the normal next Think after the actual own-selected positive-ASK correct-link insertion, before fetching/inserting the answer. The answer must remain unfetched and another read must be available. Record insertion provenance and future read availability; gold-preloaded or teacher-forced insertion cannot qualify as an own fetch. Capture existing scores without calling `_step` again: it mutates state. Distinguish fixed-loop opportunities from opportunities actually permitted by learned HALT.

**Filtering, cost, holdout.** Oracle correctness gates are training-only. Log eligible/attempted pairs, exclusion reasons, and coverage by relation/stage. Evaluate **all** chains without an oracle filter or substituted bridge; conditional metrics accompany unfiltered outcomes. Stop-gradient saves no forward pass: reuse identical same-step paired scores across arms or charge extra encoding/Think/scoring, masks and retained tensors. Refresh targets after weight changes. Ordinary one-hop losses still backpropagate. Audit the augmented manifest before target construction: never introduce A2(F(x)), its teacher-forced continuation, bridge-conditioned auxiliary pair or KL target; ordinary A2 facts/negative candidates stay eligible. Fresh A2 worlds remain instances of an already observed shift. Correct teacher argmax does not certify soft probabilities; low coverage or loss-only improvement cannot establish transfer.

**Stronger cheap control.** Same-gated extra ordinary second-fetch evidence CE is mandatory beside the same-pair/no-KL control: the existing remaining-evidence target already identifies the answer card in this two-card toy. Match additional loss opportunities/weight tuning and report equal-example plus measured equal-compute views. If KL cannot outperform this control, the evidence supports more supervision, not distinctive cross-position distribution reuse.

**Challenge to the join.** 01 already prioritizes the repeated reader; retain that ordering. Its built-in contraction supplies the composition algorithm. A win would establish a useful inductive bias, not discovery of a reusable join procedure. Give the repeated reader identical learned grounding and supervision, and compare H2 within its same-evidence regime. Fixed supplied-card join success cannot establish autonomous next-query retrieval. H2 avoids new table machinery, but its paired passes may cost more: neither H2 nor repeated reading is declared cheaper without measurement. The join earns priority only through gains beyond these simpler controls at matched total cost, with subject/link interventions and fresh A2-role transfer. No new mechanism, run, or budget is added by this critique.
