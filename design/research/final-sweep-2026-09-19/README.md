**The decision.** Keep the small custom reasoner, but establish which operation fails before adding machinery. Copying a supplied fact now works. Choosing the right fact among distractors remains weak, and the second lookup often requests the wrong relation. Those are different failures. The strongest new training hypothesis is factual sensitivity/invariance training, conditional on the existing pooling study. Shared repeated lookup comes first when choosing works but query reuse fails. Exact-text access is a fallback only when the model selected the right evidence but cannot use its compressed values.

**A concrete example.** Suppose Mira's friend is Oren, Oren's shoes are blue, and Lila's shoes are red. “What colour are Mira's friend's shoes?” requires finding Oren, then applying the ordinary shoe-colour lookup to Oren. Copying “blue” from the only supplied attribute card does not show either operation. Choosing blue among red and green alternatives tests binding: keeping track of which value belongs to which person and relation. Asking for Oren's location instead of his shoe colour is a separate query error. More detailed storage cannot supply a relation the query never requested.

**Three ranked, conditional recommendations.** These are branches selected by observed failure, not a queue of additions.

**1. Finish or inspect the already planned pool controls.** Mean pooling is a valid simple comparator because it averages the same contextual reader states within the same line boundaries, with the same history and metadata. Contextual states can encode order; this is not raw-word averaging or a line-local model. A cheaper-looking reducer still needs measured cost.

Keep objectives separate. Answer-only supplied-card choosing compares original versus mean. Evidence-supervised retrieval compares original, mean, and separate pools with every loss and input privilege matched. Under supplied-card answer-only training, the separate key is unused by the decoder; adding it supplies no new answer pathway. Do not spend a third answer-only arm expecting otherwise. Optional decoder key exposure was already proposed in the [overnight note](/Users/ben-hannan/Desktop/projects/beautiful-model/design/research/2026-09-19-overnight-research-note.md:201); it is a separate intervention, not this sweep's discovery.

Measure actual reading, decoy choosing, retrieval coverage, and second-fetch person/relation. Better probes alone cannot justify promotion. Separate pools add 33 parameters at toy width 32, not 33 stored numbers per card; their inference speed is unmeasured. Keep reader, curriculum, initial weights, card order, evidence labels, loop/read counts, optimizer, and selection rule matched. If pool results already exist, inspect them and select a branch; do not rerun merely to satisfy a newer handoff.

**2. Match the learning intervention to the remaining failure.**

**Choosing remains weak: H1 is the strongest new training hypothesis for this project.** Build a verified triplet for the same question. In original world `x`, Mira's friend Oren has blue shoes and Lila has red shoes. Irrelevant sibling `u` changes Lila's shoes while the answer remains blue. Relevant sibling `v` swaps Oren's and Lila's shoe values, making the answer red while preserving the original word inventory. Generate complete valid worlds, balance names and values, and verify answers. Correct answers must track ownership rather than merely the presence of “blue.”

Train all three examples with ordinary cross-entropy, meaning a penalty for assigning low probability to the correct answer. Add Jensen–Shannon agreement between `p_x` and `p_u`, plus a margin on the change in answer log odds:

`Δ = log[p_x(a)/p_x(b)] − log[p_v(a)/p_v(b)]`

`extra loss = λ JS(p_x,p_u) + μ max(0,m−Δ)`

Here `a` is the original answer and `b` the changed answer. The mandatory comparator receives **exactly the same triplets, targets, and minibatches with ordinary cross-entropy only**. Thus grouped CE and grouped CE plus paired loss isolate the objective. A shuffled-CE arm using the same examples, budget permitting, distinguishes grouping effects. Freeze coefficients using development data; charge all example passes, verification, and loss computation. No teacher is needed. [Learning report](/Users/ben-hannan/Desktop/projects/beautiful-model/design/research/final-sweep-2026-09-19/03-learning.md).

The closest inspected language precedents are [Kaushik et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/1909.12434), training on originals plus minimally label-changing counterfactuals, and [Teney et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2004.09034), adding gradient-based paired supervision against a same-augmented-data comparator. Teney's sensitivity objective is closely related but adds backward-pass cost and differs from this finite invariant-pair agreement. Neither inspected paper uses this exact JS-plus-changed-margin combination; that limited check proves no global novelty. H1 is an untested project transfer of established augmentation, consistency, and ranking ideas. Medical [CF-SimCLR](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.09605v2) is only a more distant matched-data precedent.

Count both answers correct on relevant pairs and both correct on invariant pairs. Merely keeping an answer stable rewards constant wrong answers. Triplet lineage and answer relationships are legitimate offline supervision, but auxiliary pair roles, oracle bridge labels, evidence IDs, and change flags must never enter model inputs, inference rewards, or test-driven selection. Disclose any extra structural selection. H1 needs a usable identity/value path and may lose to ordinary CE. Its answer losses cannot backpropagate through detached top-k indices into retrieval scores or the binary ASK decision; shared-feature changes can affect retrieval indirectly. Choosing improvement is not a retrieval or composition result.

**Choosing works but query reuse fails: try the shared reader first.** Carry the fetched person pointer and requested relation into the same learned lookup used for standalone questions. Use a deliberately narrow split: train `A0(x), A1(x), A2(x), A0(F(x)), A1(F(x))`; test `A2(F(x))` in fresh worlds after freezing development choices. `F` is the friend lookup; all three attributes, including A2, must be learned standalone. Hold wording fixed. This tests fixed-order composition-role transfer, not a new primitive, reversed order, deeper reasoning, or new program topology. Historical A2-chain results have already been observed: newly frozen worlds are fresh instances of a known shift, not an untouched composition family. [Reasoning report](/Users/ben-hannan/Desktop/projects/beautiful-model/design/research/final-sweep-2026-09-19/01-reasoning.md).

H2 is a later **privileged query-reuse diagnostic**. Pair a permitted chain with its standalone suffix, such as “Oren's shoe colour.” After a correct first link fetch, align the chain's second-fetch score distribution with the correct standalone first-fetch distribution, over identical eligible cards including NULL. A stop-gradient teacher KL term acts before detached selection. Controls receive identical paired examples, evidence supervision, eligibility gates, and loss opportunities. Besides grouped ordinary training, compare extra ordinary evidence cross-entropy at that same second fetch: more emphasis on the already-correct target may explain a gain without distribution matching. Charge standalone teacher passes and report eligibility plus unconditional results. Never train an A2 chain or its auxiliary alignment. H2 adds structural training privilege even though no bridge entity is inserted at inference.

**H2 technical note for a later experiment.** Fork chain and suffix independently from the **same label-free fact prefix**, with separate question/Think states. Never put the oracle-derived suffix before the chain: the causal reader could carry its bridge identity through question history. Share only verified prefix computation, keeping entity mappings, causal cutoffs, and age buckets aligned. Audit the augmented manifest: exclude A2(F(x)) chains, teacher-forced continuations, and bridge-conditioned auxiliary pairs. Ordinary A2 facts can remain negative candidates; deleting them changes the task.

Capture student scores on the next Think step after the actual **own-selected, positive-ASK correct-link insertion**, before answer-card insertion. Record insertion provenance, confirm the answer remains unfetched, and require another retrieval opportunity. Gold-preloaded and teacher-forced insertions are ineligible. Capture the existing forward's scores; do not call `_step` again to inspect them because it mutates the episode.

Use the chain's eligible-card mask, excluding **all previously fetched real cards, including evicted ones**, while retaining NULL. Align by stable source identities rather than assumed equal indices. Mask both vectors before softmax and renormalize on the common support. Apply teacher correctness filtering to that actual distilled distribution; report native standalone competence separately. Use valid coordinates for KL, and do not let a shadow alignment mask change ordinary standalone training.

Stop-gradient saves no teacher forward. Reuse same-step standalone scores in every arm when available; otherwise charge teacher encoding, Think, scoring, and live activations. Refresh targets after weight updates; ordinary standalone losses still backpropagate. Match the extra-evidence-CE control's eligibility, examples, loss opportunities, and tuning allowance. KL must beat that stronger control to support distinctive distribution reuse. H2 trains scores directly, not ASK/HALT. Report ranking, actual fetches, halting, both-facts recall, eligibility, and full-answer accuracy separately. Oracle correctness filtering is training-only; evaluate all chains without it. A lower KL is not a repaired retrieval policy. These safeguards come from the [final H2 audit](/Users/ben-hannan/Desktop/projects/beautiful-model/design/research/final-sweep-2026-09-19/00-evidence.md); H2 is not in the immediate pool prompt.

A built-in relational join remains exploratory after the simpler reader. It supplies the matching algorithm as an inductive bias; it does not demonstrate learned discovery. The join and repeated reader must share the same learned grounding interface and evidence. Neither receives oracle tables. Latent program search adds spurious-solution risk and candidate-execution cost; a fixed two-operation task cannot establish discovery of program structure.

**3. Reread selected evidence only when retrieval is already correct.** Memory and efficiency converge on one fallback: bounded access to the ordered text behind selected cards when their pooled values remain unusable. This cannot repair the present missing second query; 1/189 both-facts retrieval does not establish readiness.

For the clean diagnostic, hold selected indices fixed and expose their original contextual token states from the same causal reader pass, versus pooling those states. Train both answer interfaces comparably. Separately evaluate isolated-line rereading as a deployment choice: it can lose pronoun antecedents and therefore does not isolate compression by itself. Predeclare a total token cap and report overflow. Charge contextual caches or any prefix expansion, and give matched C*/E-long controls the same available context. Report full-set results alongside the correctly-retrieved subset and its denominator; count expansion across the whole episode. Positional pooling remains a cheap possible control, not another main recommendation. **Costs, objections, and claims that remain open.** Measure episode encoding plus query search, rereading, reasoning, and decoding for 1/10/100 queries. Include source tokens, offsets, caches, optimizer state, preparation, failed trials, and real versus padded exposure. Memory's illustrative 86× figure compares addressing dot-product MACs only; it predicts neither wall time nor whole-system cost. H-Net's up-to-about-2× training slowdown is implementation-specific. The ACL compressed-memory time-to-first-token (TTFT) speedups assume offline-precomputed embeddings and exclude fresh-context encoding; they are not end-to-end Premonition forecasts. No headline gain transfers automatically. [H-Net efficiency discussion](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.07955v2); [ACL timing conditions, Appendix J](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.682.pdf#page=16).

Paraphrase agreement becomes relevant only after a measured wording gap under matched facts and depth; today's fixed-wording failures do not establish that gap. Predictive world models and test-time training do not target the present evidence. Retention and rule-boundary replay wait until acquisition and forgetting are demonstrated; uniform replay is the first later comparator.
