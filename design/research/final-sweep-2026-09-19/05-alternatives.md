# Alternatives: task targets before additional machinery

Research-only outside-view report, 2026-09-19. Both supplied attachments were read first, then checked against current design and saved artifacts. No experiments, implementation changes, remote access, spending, installations, messages, or further agents. Prior council assignments supply no independent findings. Recommendations are conditional designs, not new budget authorization.

**Decision.** Preserve the custom small reasoner. Its immediate opponent should be its own simpler, properly taught version. Recommend two investigations, in order: (1) question-centred training with a tightly controlled paraphrase-consistency extension; (2) only after reliable fact selection, a small latent program controller against the already proposed direct-query controller. Reject latent world-model pretraining as the next intervention. Better task targets and separated evaluation axes currently offer more diagnostic value than another large architectural addition; whether they offer larger accuracy gains remains unmeasured.

**Six methods/limitations examined closely after the broad search.**

1. **ReCOGS: representation changes can masquerade as reasoning changes.** Wu, Manning and Potts separate semantic interpretation from incidental output punctuation, position-tied variable names and length extrapolation. Their roughly 4M Transformer is relevant in scale, but its semantic-parsing training is not village reasoning. Twenty seeds and distinct lexical/structural results matter: removing punctuation alone does not solve structural generalization. Crucially, their critique of variable-free forms shows that an easier representation can accidentally erase genuine binding requirements. Keep entity identity, event order and fresh-name demands. [Primary paper, §§3–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.13716v2).

2. **Neuro-Symbolic Concept Learner (NS-CL): executable composition without gold programs.** A learned question parser selects a program in a designed language; differentiable operators execute it over object features, with final-answer learning and a staged curriculum. This is stronger evidence for a small explicit interface than for unconstrained emergent reasoning. It supplies a domain-specific language and visual object proposals; main experiments also use pretrained perception. Spurious programs can answer correctly accidentally, motivating checks across many scenes. Dynamic events and arbitrary language are not solved by the CLEVR result. [Primary paper, §3 and appendices D–F](https://arxiv.org/html/1904.12584v1).

3. **LeWorldModel (LeWM): stable predictive latents.** Its encoder and action-conditioned predictor minimize next-latent error plus SIGReg, which regularizes random projections toward Gaussian distributions. Joint training needs neither an EMA teacher nor pixel reconstruction. Its approximately 15M-parameter, single-GPU result concerns visual control. Anti-collapse addresses a constant representation, not guaranteed retention of every rare relation. The authors explicitly limit planning horizons and warn about low-diversity data with low intrinsic dimension. [Primary paper, §3 and limitations](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.19312v1).

4. **LeWM reproduction: a sharper contrary test.** An August 2026 independent preprint reports that evaluation horizon and goal construction substantially alter results; one-step prediction error fails to rank long-horizon planners. Its evidence is narrow: one seed, fifty episodes per planning cell, and context-length differences confound mechanism attribution. It weakens the inference “better latent prediction means better reasoning”; it does not refute world models. [Primary reproduction, §§5.3, 7](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.10145v1).

5. **Test-time training (TTT): adaptation is real work and uses different information.** Akyürek et al. construct leave-one-out tasks from supplied demonstrations, train task-specific LoRA adapters, and aggregate augmented predictions. Their 1B–8B pretrained models and demonstration outputs are unlike this from-scratch, label-free setting. The appendix reports approximately twelve A100-hours for 100 ARC tasks including adaptation/inference. Results use pass@2 and some combinations include program synthesis. This is not evidence for a cheap 2M-model upgrade; weight updates also violate the present read-only evaluation contract. [Primary revised paper, §§3–4 and appendix C](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.07279v2).

6. **Reusable-procedure libraries: require actual reuse.** The EACL 2026 re-evaluation of LEGO-Prover, TroVE and AgentOptimizer measures both computational cost and whether a learned routine helps multiple problems. Many improvements shrink under matched compute; LEGO-Prover's apparent reuse is often one-problem refinement. The Llama3.1 exception retains an advantage without demonstrating library learning, so “all gains vanish” is too strong. Subset evaluation and imperfect semantic-reuse measurement limit the criticism. DreamCoder-style library growth is already in this project's research; retrieving more procedures is premature before one learned procedure transfers. [Primary paper, §§3–7](https://aclanthology.org/2026.eacl-long.163.pdf).

**Candidate 1 — paired question meanings, ordinary answer targets. Recommend first.**

For a student: learn that “Where is Mira?” and “Which place is Mira in?” ask the same thing, while changing Mira's location must change both answers. This separates understanding a question from remembering the story.

Use the existing planned label-free, question-centred objective as the starting competitor: visible prefix through the current question, current answer as the only target. The extension uses two train-only renderings of the same prefix differing solely in current-question wording. Add a fixed-weight Jensen–Shannon agreement loss between answer-token distributions, conditioned on the same training answer prefix, to their ordinary cross-entropies. Bind entities identically from visible text. No semantic type, evidence set, proof trace or canonical question is supplied at inference. Both renderings must preserve meaning; generate them from audited train templates, not an unverified LLM paraphraser.

Closest prior art is consistency regularization/data augmentation; ReCOGS supplies the representation warning, not a proof this particular loss works. Question-centred training and factorized evaluation already exist in the spec. The paired objective is new to the reviewed project material, with no worldwide novelty claim.

**Distinct prediction:** versus identical two-rendering data trained with cross-entropy alone, agreement training improves *both versions correct* on unseen wording at familiar depth, without erasing responses to changed facts. It need not improve withheld compositions under fixed wording. That asymmetric prediction separates lexical alignment from “better reasoning.”

Costs: two prefix/answer passes and distribution comparison; the control receives the same two renderings, answer exposures and total measured budget. No extra inference state or pretrained weights. Existing answer labels suffice; renderer pairing adds data-construction knowledge, not hidden semantic labels. Runtime is unknown until measured.

Small decisive screen: familiar depth 1–3, fresh bindings, crossed familiar/unseen wording; compare plain question-centred training with the paired loss using identical data. Maintain a separately identified familiar-wording diagnostic rather than replacing the primary test. Reject the extension if it only makes two wrong answers agree, fails to beat cross-entropy at matched cost, or harms relevant-change sensitivity. If plain answer-focused training already resolves the gap, adopt that simpler option. If shallow familiar wording still fails, wording was not the main bottleneck.

**Candidate 2 — a typed latent program controller. Recommend only after selection works.**

For a student: write “find Mira's friend; read that person's colour” as two reusable instructions instead of rediscovering the entire answer pattern. This targets the second-hop relation habit, not card storage.

Keep a common repaired writer and learned one-hop reader. Introduce a tiny question parser choosing a bounded program over `READ_ENTITY(pointer, relation)` and `READ_VALUE(pointer, relation)`; arguments are predicted from visible question tokens. A typed pointer distribution passes between calls. For the two-hop screen enumerate a fixed small candidate set and train the marginal answer likelihood, `-log Σz p(z|question) p(answer|execute(z,cards))`. No gold programs, entity/relation annotations or oracle facts supervise this parser. Inference selects one program from its learned probability, with frozen weights and no checker access. Learned reads remain fallible: an executable trace is not a verified proof.

NS-CL is closest prior art. The new-to-project part is answer-trained latent program selection, beyond the already proposed factorized second query; scientific novelty is unproved. Hand-designed types are an inductive bias, disclosed and shared with the comparator.

**Simplest fair competitor:** the same learned reader unrolled twice, explicitly carrying the fetched pointer and question relation, without a discrete program bottleneck. Match calls, facts, training answers and budget. Comparing only with the broken original would not isolate program structure.

**Distinct prediction:** the typed program representation improves familiar-operation/new-combination accuracy beyond this direct-query control, while one-hop accuracy remains matched. Screen the withheld relation-at-hop-two task plus role reversals, irrelevant changes and changed links. Fail if the direct-query control matches it, if correctness depends on oracle parsing, or if programs exploit decoys. Correct answers alone cannot certify correct programs; the same predicted procedure must survive new factual bindings and interventions.

Costs: parser parameters, candidate executions during training, transient pointer vectors and program tokens. With B candidates, H reads, N cards and d-dimensional scoring, lookup work is approximately O(BHNd), excluding the parser/reader. Inference has one program's H calls. Enumeration grows quickly; cap it before any funded screen. No persistent procedure library, replay or test-time optimizer. This cannot proceed while common one-hop reads are unreliable.

**Candidate 3 — event-conditioned latent prediction. Plausible, ultimately reject now.**

For a student: predict the internal picture after a narrated action rather than predict every word. A text adaptation could learn `z_next = f(z_prefix, encoded_action)` against the next visible-prefix embedding, with SIGReg and the normal answer objective. Hidden simulator states/actions would be privileged diagnostics only; public action sentences are required. This largely overlaps the project's JEPA/knock-out direction, so it is not new here or claimed novel globally.

Compare with the same reader doing ordinary next-line prediction plus answer training at equal cost. The distinctive prediction would be better answers after relevant action changes, including longer event chains, not merely lower latent error. A future decisive screen would keep narration simple, independently vary chain length, and score both changed and invariant twins. Reject on good prediction loss without improved causal answers.

This requires another prediction head, target-encoding work, batch regularization and potentially latent rollout state. Timing is unknown. Narration is only a partial observation; predicting its embedding may emphasize wording or omit rarely queried facts. Anti-collapse cannot repair those semantics. Current bottlenecks concern exact selection and question composition, while the original LeWM evidence is visual and the reproduction undermines its proposed proxy metric. Those are concrete reasons to defer it despite its appeal.

**Pretraining and decision discipline.** Qwen is relevant because the new attachment used it to write/check patterns, not because it demonstrated village reasoning. The recorded teacher-frame calibration accepted six good and rejected four of six bad examples—too small and imperfect to make it an oracle. A frozen pretrained reference could later diagnose wording under identical label-free prefixes, but its pretraining, weights and inference cost belong in a separate resource tier; it cannot establish custom-model efficiency. No Qwen execution or replacement is proposed.

Any future screen preserves versioned v2/preprocessing identities, fingerprint-enforced read-only scoring, privileged diagnostics separate from primary results, three precommitted finalist seeds, paired visit-clustered 99% bounds, coverage floors, leak exclusions, and untouched final tests. The overnight toy's `eval()`/`no_grad()` checks are weaker than the primary fingerprint wrapper. Report train/inference compute, real versus padded tokens, memory and all failed trials. Existing pilot test exposure cannot be undone. No runtime, gain or new experiment allowance is assumed. Opus retains implementation ownership.

**Cross-critique — conditional ranking revised, 2026-09-19.** Read the available [evidence audit](/Users/ben-hannan/Desktop/projects/beautiful-model/design/research/final-sweep-2026-09-19/00-evidence.md), [learning report](/Users/ben-hannan/Desktop/projects/beautiful-model/design/research/final-sweep-2026-09-19/03-learning.md), and [reasoning report](/Users/ben-hannan/Desktop/projects/beautiful-model/design/research/final-sweep-2026-09-19/01-reasoning.md), without polling. This supersedes “recommend first” as an immediate cross-lane priority: neither of my two additions is ready to outrank the existing writer/choosing controls.

**Paraphrase agreement is deferred to a measured wording bottleneck.** The current ladder already fails with fixed question syntax; agreeing across phrasings cannot supply a missing usable person–relation association. Both versions can confidently choose the same decoy. Nor does answer-distribution agreement supply a direct gradient through detached hard ASK selection. My proposal therefore makes no justified prediction of repairing current choosing or second-query reuse. Promote it only if a valid label-free model reliably answers familiar wording but fails paired, meaning-equivalent unseen wording with facts, depth, bindings and evidence availability held fixed. Ordinary question-centred supervision and identical-data cross-entropy augmentation come first. [ReCOGS](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.13716v2) motivates this separation, not agreement regularization itself.

For the measured **150/256 irrelevant-change flips**, learning-report H1 is more directly targeted than my paraphrase loss: it varies factual bindings while preserving or changing the required answer. H1 still needs a usable identity path; its matched-example/grouping controls must distinguish a special objective from ordinary augmentation. Improved supplied-card choosing would not establish improved hard retrieval. I rank this factual intervention screen above paraphrase agreement for the present failure, conditional on the writer controls.

**Typed programs move to reserve behind the repeated reader.** In the bounded friend-then-attribute task, type constraints nearly fix the program skeleton. Remaining choices are entity/relation grounding—the same decisions the factorized repeated reader already makes. A discrete bottleneck may change optimization, but “programs compose” is not a distinct demonstrated advantage. Promote only after the shared reader reliably chooses among decoys yet retains a reproducible second-hop relation/person gap, and the direct-query control fails to resolve it. The reasoning report's bounded join is a more specific alternative for this restricted composition: it eliminates the second search choice, while still depending on learned bindings. Neither deserves an oracle parser.

Answer-only program marginalization can reward a spurious route that happens to return the correct value; the sole-attribute shortcut is a concrete local example. Moreover, high summed probability over successful candidates need not make the single most probable program successful. Score the selected program, not oracle best-of-B, and require transfer across fresh bindings, changed links and distracting values. [NS-CL's spurious-program discussion](https://arxiv.org/html/1904.12584v1) supports this concern, not a guarantee that curriculum removes it.

My O(BHNd) expression hides the main search risk: with m legal operation/argument choices per step, unconstrained B can grow as m^H. Types prune but do not guarantee tractability. A fixed two-hop cap avoids an open-ended search claim; all candidate executions and gradients count. Abandon the addition if its advantage vanishes against the repeated reader at measured cost, requires privileged traces, or exists only under candidate-oracle scoring.

**Resulting order:** usable binding/choosing controls first; factual sensitivity training if decoy interference persists; factorized repeated reads before any program search if next-query reuse fails; paraphrase agreement only for an independently established wording gap. Latent world-model pretraining remains rejected for this stage. Runtime and seed/visit-clustered uncertainty remain unmeasured; no experiments or additional budget were initiated.

**Control precision from Hubble's follow-up.** Decoder access to retrieval keys is already proposed in the [overnight note](/Users/ben-hannan/Desktop/projects/beautiful-model/design/research/2026-09-19-overnight-research-note.md:201), though absent from the prepared implementation; it is not novel. Under supplied-card answer-only training, separating an unused key pool introduces no new answer pathway, so do not expect that change alone to repair choosing. The minimal retrieval comparison is original/mean/separate pooling under the same evidence-supervised objective. The separate answer-only choosing comparison starts with original versus mean. Mean must pool the **same contextual reader states over the same line boundaries**: it retains the same history access and is neither a raw-token average nor D-local. Any later identity-channel exposure must be matched across controller competitors and isolated from the pooling comparison.
