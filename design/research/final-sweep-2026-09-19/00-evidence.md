## Mechanism: strong evidence, important contrary evidence

In `exp1/card_content_probe-init-nothink+qread-s0-cur600-4000-exp2_bypass-k1-s0-4000-longread-exp2_bypass-k1-s0-1500.json`, answer-only training leaves card-person decoding **116/2048**, card-relation **656/2048**, and card-value **2048/2048**. `exp1/pool_attention_probe.json` shows **97.9%** pooling mass on the value token. Retrieval-trained default seed 0 instead puts **71.0%** on relation, **1.6%** on value. Reader states at the person and value positions retain perfect respective token decoding (**2304/2304**).

Plain explanation: the model reads the whole address label, then writes a note containing mostly the answer or mostly the address. Either can make the next operation fail. However, linear-probe failure establishes poor linear accessibility, not complete erasure or a theorem that one vector cannot encode both. Initialization already encodes all fields well; long-run cards preserve both imperfectly. The probes fit on validation visits 0–127 and score 128–255, with fixed representations and LBFGS linear classifiers. Their successful token-position probes partly measure retention of the token present at that position, not relational understanding.

The prepared two-pool implementation in `premonition/card_pools.py` has **no trained result found in the audited artifacts**. It adds `d+1` parameters: 33 at toy width 32, **129** at production width 128. Stored key/value sizes stay unchanged. A second pooling pass and training activations cost work; runtime remains unknown. Crucially, `model.py:446` inserts **value + age + row type**, not the retrieval key, into card rows; entity mentions also update slots separately. Consequently, separate key/value pools might repair retrieval while leaving supplied-card choosing unresolved. Do not promise that the decoder gains access to a card's new key.

## Append-only follow-up: fairness of writer controls and identity access

Requested narrow source review, 2026-09-19. No tests, code changes, other specialist reports, training or budget use. This addition qualifies the preceding recommendation; it does not replace prior evidence.

**Fixed mean is a fair input-information control for this writer.** `premonition/model.py:171` and `premonition/card_pools.py:50` group exactly the same token positions by `batch.line_of`, excluding positions with negative line IDs. Both pool the existing contextual reader output for the whole valid line, including its markers/newline as mapped by that same batch. Both pass their pooled d-vector through the same d→key_dim and d→d projections and the same `CardStore.write`. The latter preserves identical card validity, line boundaries, entity metadata, NULL handling and key normalization. The mean variant supplies all-zero scores, producing uniform weights. It adds no text, future question, oracle parse, role labels or extra card rows. Key width and value width are unchanged.

The reader remains the same causal minGRU/local-attention stack, with the same prior-visit-text access; pooling at line completion is allowed in both variants. Thus mean pooling contextual states is **not** mean pooling raw token embeddings: contextual states can encode order and history. It also does not make cards line-local. Later cards may still contain earlier facts in every arm. After end-to-end training, reader weights and states can diverge across arms; fairness means matching their initial state, input access and optimization protocol, not claiming identical learned representations. A frozen-reader comparison would answer a narrower question and must freeze every arm equally.

Mean keeps the old `pool` module allocated but unused. Report allocated versus active parameters and actual optimizer state, rather than claiming either an extra information channel or exactly matched effective capacity. Its aggregation and backward costs differ from learned pooling. Current `MeanPoolCardWriter` still routes zero scores through the shared softmax/scatter helper, so do not assume an optimized mean implementation's speed. No runtime saving has been measured.

**Why split pools alone may not repair choosing.** `CardStore.gather` returns values, entity mentions and line indices, not retrieval keys. `model.py:446` constructs card rows from value + age + row type. The decoder's own cross-attention key projection is applied to those rows; it is a different object from the store's retrieval key. The separate key can therefore find the right card without revealing that card's identity to a decoder choosing among supplied value rows. Entity mentions also update identity-specific slots through the binder; this is an existing indirect identity route, so “the decoder has no identity whatsoever” would overstate the concern. Several relations may update the same slot, and the binder receives the value rather than a relation-labelled triple; this route is not a guarantee of card-specific selection.

There is a sharper control implication. In the supplied-card ladder's `loss_of` (`scripts/premonition_ovn_ladder.py:316`), only answer cross-entropy is optimized; supplied IDs are selected independently of retrieval scores. Separating an otherwise unused key pool leaves the learned value-pooling function class unchanged. With identical initialization, data, random order and optimizer settings, its answer-relevant gradients should match the original single pool in this regime, apart from implementation/numerical effects. This is a source-level inference, not a tested equivalence. Evidence-supervised ASK is the regime where separating key/value gradients can remove an actual competing loss. Do not spend a full answer-only sweep expecting the unused key channel to become a new choosing mechanism.

**Identity-bearing readout is already proposed.** The overnight research note, `design/research/2026-09-19-overnight-research-note.md:201`, explicitly says: “Optionally, give the decoder's card row the key as well”. Its candidate-A criticism also states that choosing is not fixed unless the key reaches the decoder. This is prior project discussion, not a new discovery of this audit. The prepared `card_pools.py:67` and concrete next-session handoff implement only separate pools and mean pooling; they do not implement that optional key exposure. The cited passage does not specify a dedicated extra identity value, projection formula, extra row, or auxiliary identity-reconstruction loss. Those are distinct design decisions, with additional capacity/supervision costs, not implicit parts of the 33-parameter proposal.

**Minimal recommended comparison:** single learned pool, fixed contextual mean, and separate key/value pools, all using the same repaired bypass architecture and the evidence-supervised retrieval objective. This directly tests the proposed training conflict. Keep top-1 retrieval, no teacher distractors and four fixed loops identical if reproducing the existing `bypass-k1` setting. Pure-gold/no-fetch reading and supplied-decoy choosing are diagnostic readouts of each checkpoint; the latter has a distribution-shift caveat if its decoys were absent during training. A separate answer-only choosing comparison needs only single versus mean initially. Do not silently combine key exposure, a new decoder, extra Think or a new curriculum with the writer comparison.

If identity exposure is later selected, compare its presence/absence while holding the pooling choice fixed, and give the control equal projection capacity, card width/count, available metadata and supervision. A d-dimensional identity/content mixture can keep stored width constant but still adds parameters and computation. An extra row/vector changes memory and attention costs. Neither is the original minimal writer comparison.

**Axes to match or report:** exact data/preprocessing/tokenizer identities; visible read history; line boundaries and card metadata; reader/decoder architecture and width; shared initial weights and data/card-order seeds; all loss terms and weights (including evidence labels, early-answer discount, per-loop decoding and halt targets); curriculum phase boundaries and teacher/own-retrieval probabilities; top-k, number/timing of reads, loops and HALT policy; optimizer, learning-rate schedule and checkpoint-selection rule. No oracle person/relation reconstruction labels for only one arm. Use a fixed-example/update view for mechanism isolation, and measured equal-FLOP plus same-device wall-time views for efficiency: different step costs mean these cannot all be equated by one nominal step count. Account for probes, evaluations, failed trials, actual non-padding tokens, store state and optimizer state. Recalibrate architecture-dependent FLOP estimates rather than reusing a fit by assumption. Primary claims still require three precommitted seeds, visit-clustered paired uncertainty and fresh test evidence. The old handoff's timing estimate, conditional seed expansion and 1,800-second cap are historical proposals, not new authorization or measured runtime for this comparison.

**A2 exclusion concerns composition roles, not every occurrence of A2.** `premonition/toy_ladder.py:139` excludes relation 2 from training two-hop questions while retaining its facts and standalone questions. Audit the augmented pair manifest, not merely the old training manifest. A2 facts as distractor/negative candidates in permitted A0/A1 queries are already part of the task; do not remove them from score support and change the benchmark. Do not tune H2 on historical held-out A2-chain scores and later present fresh worlds of that same tuned exclusion as an untouched composition-selection test.

**Prevent bridge leakage through the prefix.** Fork the chain and suffix from the same label-free fact prefix, with independently initialized question/Think states. Do not append the oracle-derived suffix before the chain within one visit: question lines are not cards, but the causal reader still reads them and could carry the bridge forward. Reuse only verified common-prefix computation, never suffix-conditioned hidden states. Keep entity mapping, causal cutoff and line-age buckets identical. Align by stable source-line identity, not coincidentally equal tensor indices from different batches.

**Define the event, not just “loop two.”** In `model.py:569`, `_step` computes scores before `_insert` at the end of the loop. For the minimal top-1/no-distractor case, take student scores on the next Think step after the first actual own-selected, positive-ASK link insertion, before choosing/inserting the answer card. The correct answer card must remain unfetched and another retrieval opportunity must exist. Gold-preloaded episodes are ineligible. Teacher-forced insertion in either teacher or mixed-own mode is not a successful own fetch: record its provenance, and exclude or report it as a distinct teacher-forced diagnostic. Do not call `_step` again just to inspect scores; it mutates the episode. Learned-HALT runs must distinguish a real second opportunity from fixed-loop diagnostics that ignore HALT.

**Use one mask for the actual KL.** Let M be the chain's current eligible real cards under the shared causal cutoff, excluding *all* previously fetched real cards, plus NULL. Apply M to both score vectors before softmax and normalize on that common support; do not merely delete the link from already-normalized teacher probabilities. `store.py:82` tracks historical fetches, including cards subsequently evicted. Never unmask those cards in the student. Compute teacher rank-correctness on this same distribution; “top card correct” is rank competence, not proof its ASK/HALT policy would actually retrieve. Use only valid coordinates for KL to avoid invalid -infinity arithmetic. A shadow mask for teacher alignment must not silently alter its ordinary standalone training episode.

**Stop-gradient does not mean free teacher work.** The teacher is the current model's standalone first-fetch score distribution, detached for this KL. When identical standalone examples already receive ordinary losses in every arm, capture/reuse those same-step scores. Otherwise charge the additional question/prefix encoding, Think, scoring, masks and live activations; no separate decoder pass is needed solely for score alignment.
