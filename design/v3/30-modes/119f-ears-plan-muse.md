## 3. Does "one frame per sentence" explain invented/no-STATE?

Partly — necessary, not sufficient. Panel arithmetic (counted): 45 sents carry >1 DISTINCT gold relation, so ≥91 triple-rows are auto-non-exact under deterministic single-frame decode; hard exact cap is 155 (one per non-empty sent). Observed exact is 23, far below 155, so relation/span errors dominate. Mechanism for the two symptoms: on the 245 empty sents any STATE pred is "invented" (134: e.g. demonym→citizenship frames on fact-free sents); on multi-gold sents the single pred matches at most one gold ("no STATE frame" 116 includes abstains on STATE golds, consistent with inverted calibration: exact conf median 0.17 vs wrong up to 0.92). Training reinforces this: WebRED shows 34,194 distinct texts, 13,997 with >1 relation across rows — identical sentences supervised with different single golds, i.e. label noise that teaches underconfidence on real sentences. Fixing decode multiplicity (top-k frames) is REJECTED for 119f: it raises writes by construction and breaks the fixed wrong-write budget.
