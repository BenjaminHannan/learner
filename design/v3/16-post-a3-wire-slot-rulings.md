## C. Question slot

**Evidence class.** The generator's fixed `[one-hop, one-hop, two-hop, two-hop]` ordering is shown in source. The small operator probe suggests a learned slot/prefix shortcut in wire s1 and weaker sensitivity in plain s0. Population prevalence, current A3 sensitivity, and exclusive failure to use LINK remain untested. “Uses slot instead of LINK” is presently too strong.

**Cheapest decisive audit — untested specification, freeze before scoring.** Use native W and native plain on all 40+40 historical checkpoints, plus all 96 A3 checkpoints to address that experiment directly. No guard/cap changes. Use **64 fresh diagnostic worlds per seed/pair**, matched within historical plain/wire seed IDs and within A3 arms. Keep these diagnostic worlds separate from certification and historical panel identities. This is a fixed-size mechanism audit, not a high-precision G_pair remeasurement.

For each world construct the same asker's one-hop and two-hop question for all three relations, and place each at each of the four question-block slots: **2 hops × 3 relations × 4 slots**. Require the asker's direct value and its friend's value to differ for each tested relation, so answers distinguish the two routes. Disclose this diagnostic conditioning. Keep story lines, facts, card bytes, asker, relation, and target question text fixed when varying its slot. At each slot use identical preceding question text for the two hop variants; choose prefix questions independently of the target hop. Remove previous answers/feedback information using the frozen label-free policy. Reset episode/workspace/fetch state for every scored target, rebuild positions from the actual sequence, and supply no gold cards or hop labels to inference. Verify unchanged story-card keys and model weights, native parity at canonical slots, and label noninterference.

Moving a target changes its causal question prefix, token position, and card age. This audit identifies dependence on that positional context; it does not isolate an absolute-position neuron. Do not move a target while quietly retaining its original q_line or age metadata. Prior question lines are not cards but still influence the causal reader.

Report for **every checkpoint, relation, hop, and slot**: first-scheduled-opportunity actual card class (including no ASK/NULL), correct asker-LINK rate, direct-attribute and endpoint rate, eventual evidence coverage, and answer accuracy. Keep the original canonical results beside them. For twin sensitivity, replay c4/c5 edits with the same slot/prefix plan on each twin; these small diagnostics earn no G_pair pass.

**Pre-fixed diagnostic decision.** In the all-40 native wire cohort, let `D_slot` be the mean increase in correct-asker-LINK selection at the first opportunity from slots 1–2 to slots 3–4, averaged equally over both actual hop types and all three relations. Confirm a material position-conditioned routing effect if **D_slot>=.10 and its paired one-sided 95% lower bound is >0**, with world variants kept together, equal seed weighting, and the usual 100,000 seed/world hierarchical resamples. Freeze the RNG namespace `TrackA-slot-audit-v1|wire|D_slot` before scoring. This is one diagnostic primary, not a reliability certificate.

Also report the LINK-versus-no-LINK effect at each fixed slot; a slot effect does not prove the content effect is zero. Report canonical versus crossed-slot answer losses separately, especially held-out relation 2. Plain and the two A3 cohorts, individual seeds, and other metrics are prespecified descriptive extensions; do not pool them into the wire primary or select only successful checkpoints. Failure of this directional primary is inconclusive about other forms of position dependence, not proof of position invariance. The primary detects the particular route reversal reported by the builder.

**If confirmed — one training change.** Construct the existing four complete question records, then uniformly permute them per training visit with an independent RNG substream. Move question metadata with the questions and rebuild q_span/q_line/answer/evidence alignment. Preserve facts, question multiset, relation exposure, exclusion of relation 2 from two-hop training, story order, model, schedule, supervision and counted compute budget. Keep twin permutation plans shared. This changes order only and introduces no gold hop flag at inference.

Evaluate future models both canonically and on slot-balanced panels. Pre-freeze a separate development contract for fixed-order versus shuffled-order training: matched fresh controls, a trained-skill gate that requires c1/c2 success across all four slots, the same exact-test/effect-floor rule, retained READS/stuck and held-out preservation checks, plus valid budgets/integrity. Do not make solving held-out relations a prerequisite for *studying* the generator fix. There is no current launch contract or power claim for that new comparison.

Uniform permutation removes the deterministic original association, not every possible prefix-count shortcut in a block with exactly two questions of each type. The crossed-prefix audit remains necessary after training; residual dependence is a failed repair to diagnose, not permission to silently add variable question counts.

**Required answer / claim scope.** Does the identical question choose a different route when moved, and does LINK change the route at the same slot? Existing c4/c5 effects can still show dependence on edited story facts at a fixed layout. They do not establish content-driven hop selection.
