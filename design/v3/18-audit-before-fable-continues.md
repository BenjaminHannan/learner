**Representational capacity:** question-independent story encoding is compatible with retrieval; the lookup operator also encodes its lines without the question. At a fact's value position, causal story attention can encode the preceding subject and relation. One stream layer can collect the question's subject/relation, and a later layer can query contextual story keys and read the value. Three layers therefore have a plausible information path for a lookup; the mask does not make it impossible. This is an architectural argument, not a proof that width 48 fits every required function or that SGD will learn it. For k outputs, autoregressive generation supplies another depth-three computation at each emitted token; the model need not perform all k lookups within one depth-three pass.

The baseline still differs: its rows are causal, whereas the operator's row encoder is bidirectional; the operator gets a separate question encoder and three recurrent read blocks, fresh canonical queries, and supplied controller features. The baseline follows the dispatcher's world/question policy, not the lookup operator's canonical pretraining record mix. Parameter count alone does not match effective computation, training distribution or optimization difficulty. [operator encoder and reads](/Users/ben-hannan/Desktop/projects/beautiful-model/scripts/premonition_token_memory.py:113), [baseline training distribution](/Users/ben-hannan/Desktop/projects/beautiful-model/.claude/worktrees/card-experiment-handoff-7c5b27/scripts/fable_baseline_transformer.py:490).
