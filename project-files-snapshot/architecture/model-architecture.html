Premonition: how the model is built

It reads, thinks in loops, then speaks. Three parts, one job each.

Today's best whole model is called B2. It starts from nothing: no borrowed brain.

Reader: each letter knows its 4 neighbours each side

The window is worth about 6 points: removing it dropped the score by 6.3 to 6.8. Ben wants more than local summaries.

Thinker today: loops exactly 8 times

It writes steps like a notepad, at most 7. Running more rounds than it trained makes it slightly worse. Size: 256 wide, 2 blocks (3.3M in total).

Test idea, untested

Thinking space: 17 vectors today, add 16 scratch

Only about 4k extra numbers. B2 already has 6 scratch controls, and zeroing them cost 1.6 points overall and 7.8 on chains (one seed), so extra space may help. Aimed at the question types with no worked steps, where B2 trails by 6 to 9 points.

Calculator today: inside the thinker's loop

It has no learned parts. Swap plus and minus in it and 99.9% of answers follow the swap. Ben wants it moved outside, see the next cards.

Talker today: short answers only, no sentences

The talker does no thinking. The thinker's state tells it what to point at.
